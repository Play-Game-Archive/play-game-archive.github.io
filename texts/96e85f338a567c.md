# 简介

任务故事希望玩家寻找什么、经历什么，关卡空间却未必能及时把这些意图表达出来。本文提出Quest to Dungeon（QtD）：设计师在6×6网格里组合空白、采集、寻找、战斗、钥匙、锁、首领和自定义环节，系统再以不同模板生成相应房间，使任务关系与空间布局一一对应。八名具有游戏开发背景的参与者，分别完成双结局任务和自由设计任务；问卷与操作日志显示，工具有助于可视化设计意图，也支持先搭布局再填任务、或先有故事再组织空间的迭代方式。生成房间不能直接编辑，控制感评价也较低；研究只测试了单个用户，因此跨设计岗位的协作效果仍是参与者的预期和后续研究目标。

## Abstract

<!-- translation -->
## 摘要
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0004:paragraph-->
Quest to Dungeon (QtD) is a tool designed to bridge the gap between narrative design and procedural level generation in games, two processes that are typically developed in isolation from each other. QtD connects narrative design with level design by enabling designers to create quests by combining tasks in a grid interface, where each narrative task automatically generates corresponding dungeon rooms using task-specific algorithms. We evaluated QtD through a user study (N=8) where all participants created first a quest with two endings, and then a quest without constraints. Our results showed that participants found the tool intuitive and effective in visualizing how narrative objectives translate into level design. QtD helped enable faster iteration cycles for the participants, who speculated about its possible benefits for collaboration and communication between narrative and level designers.

<!-- translation -->
Quest to Dungeon（QtD）是一种旨在弥合游戏中叙事设计与程序化关卡生成之间鸿沟的工具；这两个过程通常彼此独立开展。QtD允许设计师在网格界面中组合任务环节来创建任务，由此把叙事设计与关卡设计连接起来；每个叙事环节都会采用针对该环节的算法，自动生成相应的地牢房间。我们通过一项用户研究（N=8）评估了QtD，所有参与者先创建一个有两种结局的任务，再创建一个不受约束的任务。结果显示，参与者认为工具直观，能够有效地把叙事目标如何转化为关卡设计呈现出来。QtD帮助参与者加快了迭代周期；他们也推测了它可能为叙事设计师与关卡设计师之间的协作和沟通带来的益处。

## 1. Introduction

<!-- translation -->
## 1. 引言
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0008:paragraph-->
Collaboration and communication challenges permeate game development at different levels and development stages [1, 2, 3]. One reason for this is how heterogeneous teams are, with developers from distinct disciplines working together towards a shared goal. Yet, they might not have a shared understanding, language, and terminology. In this paper we present Quest to Dungeon (QtD), a tool developed for quest-constrained generation focusing on how manually placed quest-task actions can influence level generation and vice-versa in early prototyping. We also focus on a significant real-world problem that persists in game development, PCG systems and narrative (and narrative elements such as quests) design are typically developed in isolation, resulting in disconnected deployment. In QtD, designers use a node grid to make quests that then are automatically turned into levels. Designers can compose a main quest and subquests with alternative routes and optional tasks. These tasks are then used to generate a proposed level layout relevant to the tasks and that interconnects them. The spatial layout of the quests and levels is the same in a 6x6 grid, which forces the level layout to track 1-to-1 the layout of the quests. The level layout, while not modifiable, is created with the possibility to be used as-is, or as a way for designers to communicate their ideas to different teams on what they imagine the level design or narrative to be. QtD’s long-term goal is to help the communication and collaboration between narrative and level designers at early production stages.

<!-- translation -->
协作与沟通挑战，贯穿游戏开发的不同层面和开发阶段[1, 2, 3]。原因之一，是团队具有高度异质性：来自不同学科的开发者，为共同目标而一起工作，但未必拥有共同的理解、语言与术语。本文介绍Quest to Dungeon（QtD），一种为受任务约束的生成而开发的工具，重点考察在早期原型制作中，手动放置的任务环节行动如何影响关卡生成，反之又如何。我们也关注游戏开发中持续存在的一个重要现实问题：PCG系统与叙事设计，包括任务等叙事要素，通常彼此独立开发，导致部署时相互脱节。在QtD中，设计师使用节点网格制作任务，然后将其自动转化为关卡。设计师可以编排主线任务和支线任务，包含替代路线与可选环节。随后，系统利用这些环节生成与之相关、并将其连接起来的建议关卡布局。任务与关卡在同一个6×6网格中具有相同的空间布局，迫使关卡布局与任务布局形成一对一对应。虽然关卡布局无法修改，但设计时考虑了两种使用可能：直接使用生成结果，或让设计师借此向不同团队传达自己对关卡设计或叙事的设想。QtD的长期目标，是在制作早期帮助叙事设计师与关卡设计师沟通、协作。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0009:paragraph-->
We focus on quest design and its implication within the overall game design, particularly the impact on level design. This is because narrative and level (or space) facets are linked and complement each other [4, 5]; thus, the design of either and their generation benefits from a more holistic design. Within research there exist many examples of these being interconnected for Procedural Content Generation (PCG) [5, 6, 7]. Similarly, the game industry has worked with similar objectives, mainly trying to either fit generated quests in the gameworld or human-authored quests in generated levels. For instance, The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim[8] infamously uses radiant quests (procedural objectives) but deploys them in generic, repetitive dungeons, creating a disconnect between narrative goals and level design [9]. This approach leads to inefficiencies: level designers constantly need to align environments with quest requirements, while narrative designers lack tools to ensure their objectives are supported by the generated content. The result is increased iteration time, inconsistent player experiences, and missed opportunities for fun replayability.

<!-- translation -->
我们关注任务设计及其对整体游戏设计的意义，尤其是对关卡设计的影响。原因是，叙事与关卡，或空间，这两个面向彼此相连、相互补充[4, 5]；因此，无论设计还是生成，都能从更整体的设计方式中受益。研究中，已有许多为程序化内容生成（PCG）而将二者互联的例子[5, 6, 7]。同样，游戏业也曾围绕类似目标开展工作，主要是试图把生成的任务嵌入游戏世界，或把人工编写的任务放进生成的关卡。例如，《The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim》[8]因使用辐射任务，即程序化目标，却将其安置在通用、重复的地牢中而广受诟病，造成叙事目标与关卡设计脱节[9]。这种做法导致效率低下：关卡设计师必须不断使环境与任务要求对齐，叙事设计师却缺少工具，来确保生成内容能够支持自己的目标。结果是，迭代时间增加、玩家体验不一致，也错失了创造有趣重玩体验的机会。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0010:paragraph-->
We evaluated the effectiveness of QtD and how it could be used through a user study (N=8) where we collected qualitative feedback and tool logs. Our results indicate that QtD was easy-to-use, helped visualise how quests and tasks could be placed in level design with faster iteration cycles. Participants also speculated about how it could be used in practice and for collaboration between departments and designers, emphasizing its role in brainstorming, setting a clearer plan, and communication.

<!-- translation -->
我们通过一项用户研究（N=8），评估QtD的有效性及其可能的使用方式，并收集了质性反馈与工具日志。结果表明，QtD易于使用，能够以更快的迭代周期，帮助人们看清任务及其环节可以怎样安排进关卡设计。参与者还推测了它在实践中，以及部门和设计师之间协作中的用途，强调了它在头脑风暴、明确计划和沟通方面的作用。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0011:heading-->
## 2. Related Work

<!-- translation -->
## 2. 相关工作
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0012:paragraph-->
Narrative is a key facet in games and has a strong relation with the gameworld, levels, and in general spaces where the game will take place [10]. Within the narrative facet and PCG, quests have been the target of a large body of research for generating them such as Questbrowser [11], a quest design tool where users can query the system for ideas and alternatives and the work by Grinblat and Bucklew [12] in “Caves of Qud”, dynamically generating quests constrained by the world and authored templates. Combining quests and level generation has also been explored with interesting results. Questgram [13] enable designers to create quests and receive recommendations on what task to add next based on their current quest and the dungeon composed of both human-authored and generated rooms. Taksim [14] focuses on combining generated mission graphs with game spaces using answer-set programming. Dormans and Bakkes [5] generated missions and spaces with grammars have also been used for Unexplored and Unexplored 2. While these systems show the possibilities of connecting the two facets, how content limits and interact with each other is unclear as well as how human-authored content could fit with generated content, which is one of the goals with QtD. Yet, significant challenges remain when trying to connect quests and levels automatically as well as the communication and collaboration challenges that exist when designing these. Mixed-initiative PCG systems could help mitigate some of these challenges by giving designers control leveraging automatic generation.

<!-- translation -->
叙事是游戏的一个关键面向，与游戏世界、关卡，以及更一般地说游戏发生的空间，有着紧密关系[10]。在叙事面向与PCG研究中，大量工作以任务生成为对象，例如Questbrowser[11]，这是一种任务设计工具，用户可以向系统查询构想与替代方案；又如Grinblat和Bucklew[12]在《Caves of Qud》中的工作，根据世界与预先编写的模板约束，动态生成任务。任务与关卡生成的结合，也已有一些有趣成果。Questgram[13]允许设计师创建任务，并根据当前任务，以及由人工制作和生成房间共同构成的地牢，推荐接下来应添加什么环节。Taksim[14]着重使用答案集编程，把生成的任务图与游戏空间结合起来。Dormans和Bakkes[5]用文法生成任务与空间的方法，也被用于《Unexplored》和《Unexplored 2》。这些系统展示了连接两个面向的可能性，但内容如何相互限制、相互作用，以及人工制作内容如何与生成内容相配合，仍不清楚；这也是QtD的目标之一。然而，无论是自动连接任务与关卡，还是设计它们时存在的沟通与协作问题，仍有重大挑战。混合主动式PCG系统，通过在利用自动生成的同时赋予设计师控制权，可能有助于缓解其中一些挑战。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0013:paragraph-->
The interest and challenge of aligning quests and levels, as well as human-authored content (either quest or levels) with automatic generation has also being the object of discussion within the game industry. Levine [15] described the process required to align BioShock’s narrative objectives with level design, while Smith and Colantonio [16] discussed Arkane Studios’ “narrative ecology” approach that places story elements within environmental design for Dishonored[17]. Games such as Hitman (2016) [18] uses “opportunity” structures that place narrative moments within level design, allowing players to discover and trigger quests through environmental interaction. The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild [19] uses “chemistry engine” principles where quest objectives interact with environmental systems, creating narrative possibilities within designed spaces.

<!-- translation -->
如何将任务与关卡对齐，以及如何将人工制作的任务或关卡，与自动生成结合起来，也一直是游戏业讨论的兴趣点和挑战。Levine[15]描述了使《BioShock》的叙事目标与关卡设计对齐所需的过程；Smith和Colantonio[16]则讨论了Arkane Studios的“叙事生态”方法，即把故事要素安置在《Dishonored》[17]的环境设计中。《Hitman》（2016）[18]等游戏使用“机遇”结构，把叙事时刻放在关卡设计之中，让玩家通过与环境互动，发现并触发任务。《The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild》[19]采用“化学引擎”原则，使任务目标与环境系统互动，在设计好的空间中创造叙事可能性。
<!-- endpair -->

## 3. Quest to Dungeon Tool

<!-- translation -->
## 3. Quest to Dungeon工具
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0015:paragraph-->
QtD takes a narrative-first approach where quest structures drive level generation, in contrast to most prior systems that combine the two facets, which integrate narrative within the level generation. QtD follows a modular architecture that separates the quest structure definition from dungeon generation, giving users full control over the quest structure, while the AI system procedurally generates the dungeons. QtD was developed in Unity using version 6000.0.36f1.

<!-- translation -->
QtD采取叙事先行的方法，由任务结构驱动关卡生成；这与此前大多数结合两个面向、却在关卡生成过程中整合叙事的系统不同。QtD采用模块化架构，把任务结构定义与地牢生成分开，用户完全控制任务结构，AI系统则以程序化方式生成地牢。QtD使用Unity 6000.0.36f1版本开发。

<!-- pair:en:QD0017:heading-->
### 3.1. Quest Structure Module

<!-- translation -->
### 3.1. 任务结构模块
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0018:paragraph-->
The quest assignment interface uses Unity’s UI system to create an interactive 6×6 grid shown in figure 3 top row. Each grid node consists of a drop-down menu containing multiple task types users can choose from. For this iteration, users can choose between: Blank - used as placeholder and no task needs to be done, Gather - tasks related to gathering resources, Find - tasks related to finding resources or quest items, Fight - tasks related to engaging in combat, Key and Lock - enable a key and lock task sequence represented as separated tasks that condition each other, Boss - task associated to final encounters and Custom - placeholder task for users to add their own quest task idea. Once a task type is selected for a node, a subsequent drop-down appears that allows users to further specialize that task type and that is related to the task itself (e.g., prototypical quest items appear for a Find task). After creating a grid node, users can add adjacent grid nodes to the previous one, linking these two nodes together. Through this graphical process, users can shape diverse sequence of tasks with optional tasks, secondary objectives, and multiple paths to complete a quest. When a user finalizes a quest structure by pressing Generate, the system then takes all the tasks and their relative position to generate the rooms and interconnect them.

<!-- translation -->
任务分配界面使用Unity的UI系统，创建一个可交互的6×6网格，如图3上排所示。每个网格节点都包含一个下拉菜单，用户可以从多种环节类型中选择。在这一迭代版本中，用户可以选择：Blank，空白，用作占位，无须完成任何环节；Gather，采集，与收集资源相关；Find，寻找，与寻找资源或任务物品相关；Fight，战斗，与参与战斗相关；Key与Lock，钥匙与锁，形成一个钥匙—锁环节序列，被表示为相互施加条件的独立环节；Boss，首领，与最终遭遇相关；Custom，自定义，作为占位环节，让用户加入自己的任务环节构想。为节点选定环节类型后，会出现另一个与该环节本身有关的下拉菜单，允许进一步细化类型，例如寻找环节会出现典型任务物品。创建网格节点后，用户可以在前一个节点旁添加相邻节点，把二者连接起来。通过这一图形化过程，用户能够塑造多样的环节序列，包含可选环节、次要目标和完成任务的多条路径。用户按下Generate，完成任务结构后，系统便利用全部环节及其相对位置，生成并连接房间。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0019:heading-->
### 3.2. Dungeon Generation Module

<!-- translation -->
### 3.2. 地牢生成模块
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0020:paragraph-->
The level layout is the same as the task graph as shown in figure 3 bottom row. The dungeon generation utilizes Unity’s tile-map system to transform all stored data into 2D tile-based rooms, using the same dungeon layout as the quest interface. Each task has an analogous level design algorithm for the creation of the room. The room generation process follows a template-based approach with controlled randomization to create visual variety while preserving functional requirements. This approach ensures that, while rooms of the same task type share common characteristics, they maintain visual distinctiveness. Each room is 25x25 tiles consisting of wall, floor, and treasure tiles as well as special key tiles for key-lock mechanisms. Patterns with these tiles such as “corridors”, “cages” and “pillars” are also generated depending on which room to add visual representation to the dungeon layout. The level cannot be edited as it is meant as the automatic projection of the set of tasks designed by the user, but can be fully regenerated at any time.

<!-- translation -->
关卡布局与任务图相同，如图3下排所示。地牢生成利用Unity的瓦片地图系统，把全部已储存数据转化为二维瓦片房间，地牢布局与任务界面一致。每种环节都有相应的关卡设计算法，用来创建房间。房间生成过程采用基于模板、带受控随机化的方法，在保留功能要求的同时，创造视觉变化。这保证了同一环节类型的房间虽然共享某些特征，却仍保持视觉上的区别。每间房间为25×25块瓦片，由墙、地板和宝物瓦片，以及用于钥匙—锁机制的特殊钥匙瓦片构成。根据房间类型，还会用这些瓦片生成“走廊”“笼状围构”和“柱子”等模式，为地牢布局增加视觉表征。关卡无法编辑，因为它被设定为用户所设计环节集合的自动投影，但随时都可以完整重新生成。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0021:paragraph-->
For each task type, we define a set of room templates that satisfy the fundamental requirements of that task, Core Structural Elements such as walls and pathways between connected rooms, and Task-specific Features such as number of enemies and item spawn locations.

<!-- translation -->
对于每种环节类型，我们都定义了一组满足其基本要求的房间模板，包括核心结构要素，例如墙壁与相连房间之间的通路，以及环节专属特征，例如敌人数量和物品生成位置。

<!-- pair:en:QD0022:heading-->
### 3.3. Room Generation

<!-- translation -->
### 3.3. 房间生成
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0023:paragraph-->
Rooms are generated based on the tasks that users manually add. Each room pattern includes specific constraints to ensure that every generated room is feasible. For rooms that include corridors, these are always fixed in size for the algorithm to guarantee navigability. For rooms that can generate pillars, additional constraints are applied to ensure that pillars are never placed adjacent to each other or to walls. The QtD tool contains eight different room types and they are exemplified in figure 1.

<!-- translation -->
房间根据用户手动添加的环节生成。每种房间模式都包含特定约束，以确保每间生成的房间都可行。对于包含走廊的房间，走廊尺寸始终固定，使算法能够保证可通行性。对于能够生成柱子的房间，还会施加额外约束，确保柱子既不与彼此相邻，也不与墙壁相邻。QtD工具包含八种不同房间类型，示例见图1。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0024:paragraph-->
Blank Pattern Room (fig. 1.a): The Blank pattern is the default task for every room. It consists only of an empty layout surrounded by walls and acts more as a room without a task.

<!-- translation -->
空白模式房间（图1.a）：空白模式是每间房间的默认环节。它只有一个四周围墙的空布局，更像是一间没有任务环节的房间。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0025:paragraph-->
Gather Pattern Room (fig. 1.b): The Gather Pattern Room creates centralized room layouts centered around a core area with radiating branches. The design features a central hub with a wall structure in its middle, connected to 3-4 main corridors that extend outward. These main corridors may branch into smaller side passages, creating exploration opportunities. Items are then placed throughout the room.

<!-- translation -->
采集模式房间（图1.b）：采集模式房间创建以核心区域为中心、分支向外辐射的集中式布局。设计有一个中央枢纽，中间是一处墙体结构，连接着向外延伸的3—4条主走廊。这些主走廊可以再分出较小的侧通道，创造探索机会。随后，物品被放置在房间各处。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0026:paragraph-->
Find Pattern Room (fig. 1.c): The Find Pattern Room generates a maze based on a modified recursive backtracking approach. The algorithm carves paths through an initially solid block, ensuring all areas remain connected while maintaining sufficient wall thickness between passages.

<!-- translation -->
寻找模式房间（图1.c）：寻找模式房间采用一种经修改的递归回溯方法生成迷宫。算法从最初的实心块中凿出路径，在通道之间保留足够墙厚的同时，确保所有区域保持连通。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0027:paragraph-->
Fight Pattern Room (fig. 1.d): The Fight Pattern Room generates layouts with an outer square boundary and an inner “cage” structure. This creates a room-within-a-room design. The inner area contains dynamically placed pillars whose number and size adapt based on the number of enemies the user chose to add to the task.

<!-- translation -->
战斗模式房间（图1.d）：战斗模式房间生成具有外部方形边界和内部“笼状”结构的布局，形成房中房的设计。内部区域包含动态放置的柱子，其数量与尺寸，会根据用户选择加入该环节的敌人数量调整。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0028:paragraph-->
Key Pattern Room (fig. 1.e): The Key pattern Room creates a layout with color-coded shapes in the center of the room. The room features a colored square in the center that is hollowed out by a specific shape chosen by the user (triangle (red), square (green), circle (blue), and hexagon (yellow)).

<!-- translation -->
钥匙模式房间（图1.e）：钥匙模式房间创建一种在房间中央放置颜色编码形状的布局。房间中央有一个有色方块，按用户选择的特定形状挖空：三角形为红色，正方形为绿色，圆形为蓝色，六边形为黄色。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0029:paragraph-->
Lock Pattern Room (fig. 1.f): The Lock Pattern Room generates rooms that restrict access to other areas. The layout features a specific color-coded shape in the center of the room chosen by the user. Shapes and colors are the same as for the key pattern rooms.

<!-- translation -->
锁模式房间（图1.f）：锁模式房间生成限制通往其他区域的房间。布局中央有一个由用户选择、以特定颜色编码的形状。形状和颜色与钥匙模式房间相同。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0030:paragraph-->
Boss Pattern Room (fig. 1.g): The Boss Pattern Room creates a spacious, symmetrical layout designed for boss encounters. The algorithm produces a square room with an outer perimeter of walls and an open central area. Within this space, eight pillars are positioned in a symmetrical pattern.

<!-- translation -->
首领模式房间（图1.g）：首领模式房间创建宽敞、对称、为首领遭遇设计的布局。算法生成一个外围是墙、中央区域开放的方形房间。在这个空间中，八根柱子以对称模式摆放。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0031:paragraph-->
Custom Pattern Room (fig. 1.h): The Custom Pattern Room consists of a blank room with a white square in the center of the room, where users can write whatever they want inside this white square. It is primarily used as placeholder of not implemented tasks.

<!-- translation -->
自定义模式房间（图1.h）：自定义模式房间由一间空白房间构成，中央有一个白色方块，用户可以在其中写下任何内容。它主要用作尚未实现的任务环节的占位符。

## 4. Experiment

<!-- translation -->
## 4. 实验
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0033:paragraph-->
We conducted a user evaluation and collected both data from surveys and data logs from the tool as participants used the tool. 我们进行了用户评估，在参与者使用工具时，收集问卷数据与工具数据日志。记录的量化指标包括总节点数、分支深度、网格使用百分比和重新生成事件。质性问卷回答由人工分析，引语来自问卷中的质性问题。研究安排首先包括一项有两名参与者的在线先导研究，他们遵循与现场参与者相同的程序，以了解工具是否需要修改、问卷是否有不清楚之处；随后是一项有八名参与者的现场用户研究，使用工具的时间最多为13分钟。我们的目标，是评估QtD的可用性、它在弥合基于任务的叙事方法与关卡设计之间鸿沟方面的有效性，并了解它如何借助自动关卡生成，充当沟通媒介。

参与者先听取项目说明，并提供知情同意。随后，他们观看屏幕上的教程弹窗，最多5分钟，了解工具功能与环节类型。评估中，他们先有3分钟创建一个具有两种可能结局的任务，以熟悉工具；接着进入5分钟自由探索阶段，创建一个不受约束的任务。

### 5.1. Data from the QtD tool

<!-- translation -->
### 5.1. QtD工具的数据
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0040:paragraph-->
Data collected from the tool logs shows the total distribution of task types across the two quest creation tasks (Quest 1 - 3 minutes, and Quest 2 - 5 minutes). For Quest 1, which consisted of users designing a dungeon with two separate endings, the Fight task was the most frequently utilized (31 instances), followed by nearly equal usage of Blank (19), Gather (19), and Find (18) tasks. Key (16), Lock (14), and Boss (13) tasks were moderately used, while the Custom task was used sparingly (5 instances). This distribution suggests that combat-focused narrative elements were prioritized in the first quest design, which is expected as the tasks are biased towards this type of quests. Quest 2, which consisted of users being able to freely explore and design a dungeon, showed a slight difference in design approach. Fight tasks still dominated but with less frequency (45 instances), a significant rise in Find tasks however occurred (39 instances compared to 18 in Quest 1). Gather tasks also increased from 19 to 26 instances. This shift could indicate that as designers gained familiarity with the tool, they incorporated more exploration and collection-based narrative elements, creating more complex quest structures.

<!-- translation -->
工具日志数据展示了两次任务创建活动中各环节类型的总分布，任务1为3分钟，任务2为5分钟。在任务1中，用户设计一个具有两个独立结局的地牢；战斗环节最常使用，共31次；空白、采集和寻找的使用量几乎相同，分别为19、19和18次。钥匙16次、锁14次、首领13次，使用程度居中；自定义环节很少使用，只有5次。这种分布表明，第一次任务设计优先采用了以战斗为中心的叙事要素；由于这些环节本就偏向这类任务，这也是预期之中的。任务2允许用户自由探索和设计地牢，设计方法略有不同。战斗环节仍占主导，但频率更低，共45次；寻找环节则显著增多，从任务1的18次升至39次。采集环节也从19次增至26次。这种转变可能表明，随着设计师逐渐熟悉工具，他们纳入了更多基于探索和收集的叙事要素，创建了更复杂的任务结构。

The average dungeon size also increased from 17.2 rooms in Quest 1 to 23.5 rooms in Quest 2, resulting in more elaborated quest structures. This suggests that creating more dense and eventful quests are more prioritized than creating linear quest lines. Lastly, since the tool allows users to switch back and forth between the quest and dungeon modules, multiple iterations of the same quests were saved each time users moved back and forth to analyse the different ways of using QtD. 工具的可用性、有效性与界面：参与者高度评价任务分配界面的直观程度，平均得分为7.88/10，标准差为1.25。大多数参与者强调其易用性和有益的可视化，认为QtD“容易理解，而且［有］非常好的可视化”（p1），以及“容易使用，我大概会在游戏开发早期使用它”（p2）。这一较高可用性评分表明，6×6网格系统与基于节点的设计，为任务设计提供了一个易于接近的框架，无须具备大量技术知识。环节包含会影响生成房间的变量，让用户拥有更多控制。八名参与者中七人认为，这些变量显著影响了自己的设计过程，平均得分为8.13/10，标准差为1.73。这表明，额外的环节专属参数，成功增强了工具的灵活性，同时维持了易用性。

For the free-roam task (Quest 2), participants demonstrated more diverse and complex design approaches. Many described creating more interconnected pathways and utilizing a broader range of task types. One participant explained, “I wanted to create a ’World of Warcraft’ type quest, that followed a linear path, and taking the player on a journey through the different tasks that were available to me.”(p6) Another mentioned focusing on “a puzzle focused dungeon with a lot of locked doors and keys.”(p3) This evolution in design complexity from Quest 1 to Quest 2 aligns with the quantitative data showing larger, more diverse dungeons in the second session.

<!-- translation -->
对于自由探索任务，即任务2，参与者展示出更多样、更复杂的设计方法。许多人描述了更相互连通的路径，以及对更广泛环节类型的运用。一位参与者解释：“我想创建一个《World of Warcraft》式的任务，沿着一条线性路径，让玩家经历我能使用的不同环节，走上一段旅程。”（p6）另一位提到，自己专注于“一座以解谜为中心、有许多锁门和钥匙的地牢”（p3）。从任务1到任务2，设计复杂性出现的这种变化，与第二轮地牢更大、更多样的量化数据相吻合。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0049:paragraph-->
Several participants described an iterative workflow where they would first establish a layout using basic tasks and then refine it. One participant explained: “Create a bunch of empty rooms, find a structure that I liked and then swapped those empty rooms into either fights, gathers, or finds, wherever I felt like it would fit.”(p2). This suggests that QtD supported a layered design process from a more draft version towards something participants envisioned in a quick iterative manner.

<!-- translation -->
几位参与者描述了一种迭代工作流：先用基本环节建立布局，再加以细化。一位参与者解释：“先创建一堆空房间，找到一个我喜欢的结构，然后在我觉得合适的地方，把空房间换成战斗、采集或寻找。”（p2）这表明，QtD支持一种分层设计过程，通过快速迭代，从较粗略的草稿走向参与者所设想的东西。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0050:heading-->
## 6. Discussion

<!-- translation -->
## 6. 讨论
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0051:heading-->
### 6.1. Integration of Narrative and Level Design

<!-- translation -->
### 6.1. 叙事与关卡设计的整合
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0052:paragraph-->
Our results show the value of coupling game facets such as narrative and level, and the potential of these to mediate design and collaborative decision-making. Users could quickly see how their quest choices affected the physical layout, allowing for immediate adjustments. The high rating for effectiveness of generated rooms that represents the tasks indicates that the translation from narrative design to level design was largely successful. QtD was able to show how participants process was influenced by the QtD workflow and that the system successfully conveyed gameplay pacing and narrative progression. QtD and similar tools could help address challenges with isolated facet development, particularly when affected by procedural systems such as side quests in Skyrim to create better gameplay.

<!-- translation -->
我们的结果展示了耦合叙事、关卡等游戏面向的价值，以及它们在设计和协作决策中充当中介的潜力。用户可以迅速看见任务选择如何影响实体布局，从而立即调整。生成房间代表任务环节的有效性获得高分，表明叙事设计向关卡设计的转化大体成功。QtD能够展示参与者的过程如何受QtD工作流影响，以及系统如何成功传达玩法节奏与叙事推进。QtD和类似工具，可能有助于应对各面向孤立开发带来的挑战，尤其是当它们受到《Skyrim》支线任务等程序化系统影响时，从而创造更好的玩法。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0053:paragraph-->
The varying effectiveness of different task pattern rooms provided insight into what designers value in procedurally generated content. The Find and Gather algorithms for example rated significantly higher than the Fight algorithm in terms of how interesting the room layout were, suggesting that designers prefer room layouts with more complex structural elements. The tool could therefore help designers to create more complex patterns automatically as a starting point that would be time-consuming to design manually, rather than simpler and more open layouts. The effectiveness of the specific room generation algorithms could also suggest how procedural techniques can enhance gameplay in the context of narrative generated rooms. For example, the Gather pattern algorithm with strategically placed collection points effectively communicates a scavenger hunt experience, while the Find pattern’s maze communicates exploration and discovery.

<!-- translation -->
不同环节模式房间的有效性差异，让我们了解设计师重视程序化生成内容的哪些方面。例如，在房间布局有多有趣这一问题上，寻找和采集算法的评分显著高于战斗算法，这表明设计师更喜欢结构要素较复杂的房间布局。因此，工具可以帮助设计师自动创建更复杂的模式，作为起点；这些模式若手工设计会很耗时，而不是提供更简单、更开放的布局。具体房间生成算法的有效性，也可能说明，程序化技术如何在叙事生成房间的情境中增强玩法。例如，采集模式算法通过策略性放置收集点，有效传达了寻宝搜集的体验；寻找模式的迷宫，则传达了探索与发现。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0054:heading-->
### 6.2. Collaborative Design Workflows

<!-- translation -->
### 6.2. 协作设计工作流
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0055:paragraph-->
Collaboration and communication challenges permeate game development [1] and tools like QtD could enable a better workflow. While QtD was evaluated with a single designer to create quests and see how generated rooms affected their design and expressed their ideas, our final goal is to explore how QtD and similar tools might transform collaborative workflows between different design specialties. First steps were taken in that direction as participants expressed that the tool could be used effectively as a communication medium, for early development phases, and planning work in teams.

<!-- translation -->
协作与沟通挑战贯穿游戏开发[1]，QtD这样的工具可能带来更好的工作流。虽然QtD的评估，是让单个设计师创建任务，观察生成房间如何影响其设计、表达其想法，但我们的最终目标，是探索QtD及类似工具，可能怎样改变不同设计专长之间的协作工作流。我们已朝这个方向迈出第一步，因为参与者表示，工具可以有效充当沟通媒介，用于开发早期，以及团队中的规划工作。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0056:paragraph-->
Further, our usability results also suggests that QtD simplifies the design process, lowering the technical barrier to expressing design intent, potentially enabling narrative designers that could have limited technical skills to communicate layout requirements effectively. This direct translation of task-types to room structures could also create a vocabulary that can be shared between narrative and level designers, potentially reducing miscommunication and iteration cycles typically required to align these two aspects of game design.

<!-- translation -->
此外，可用性结果也表明，QtD简化了设计过程，降低了表达设计意图的技术门槛，可能使技术能力有限的叙事设计师，也能有效传达布局要求。从环节类型到房间结构的直接转化，还可能创造一种叙事设计师与关卡设计师共享的词汇，从而减少为对齐这两个游戏设计方面，通常需要的沟通误差和迭代周期。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0057:heading-->
### 6.3. User Development Process

<!-- translation -->
### 6.3. 用户的开发过程
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0058:paragraph-->
The data logs showed that most users had an iterative approach to quest development. Users frequently moved back and forth between the quest and dungeon modules, making adjustments to their quest structures after seeing the generated layouts (seen in figure 3). This iterative process suggests that the visual feedback from the dungeon generation could have influenced quest design decisions. Participants approached the quest development using both a layout-first and narrative-first approach. Some participants began by establishing a physical dungeon layout of empty rooms that was later populated with appropriate tasks. One participant described it as: “I just clicked out rooms in a random pattern then I connected the different branches into a flow that would seem nice. After that I began choosing different rooms that would fit together to create a nice game experience.” (p8). However, some users took a narrative-first approach instead, conceptualizing the quest story before considering dungeon layout. A participant described: “My idea for the quest was already in my mind when I started to build the level, so the quest did influence the level but not as much the other way around.” (p4). This shows that the QtD tool can be flexible enough to be utilized in different design approaches.

<!-- translation -->
数据日志显示，大多数用户采用迭代方法开发任务。他们经常在任务与地牢模块之间来回移动，看到生成布局后，再调整任务结构，如图3所示。这种迭代过程表明，地牢生成的视觉反馈可能影响任务设计决策。参与者既采用布局先行，也采用叙事先行的方法。一些人先建立由空房间组成的实体地牢布局，再填入合适环节。一位参与者这样描述：“我只是按随机模式点出一些房间，再把不同分支连接成一种看起来不错的流程。然后，我开始选择能放在一起、创造良好游戏体验的不同房间。”（p8）不过，有些用户采取了叙事先行的方法，在考虑地牢布局之前，就先构思任务故事。一位参与者描述：“开始搭建关卡时，我心里已经有任务的构想，所以任务确实影响了关卡，但反过来的影响没有那么大。”（p4）这显示，QtD能够灵活到足以用于不同设计方法。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0059:heading-->
### 6.4. Limitations and Future Work

<!-- translation -->
### 6.4. 局限与未来工作
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0060:paragraph-->
Our evaluation showed the possibilities of using QtD to bridge quest design and level design. Participants emphasized its possible use to help communication and collaboration between designers as they speculated about it, and gave us a good insight into how the tool is used, its effectiveness and the design process. Yet since we only tested with single users and not with a team, we cannot conclude its usability as a communication and collaboration tool, which would be the next step for this project.

<!-- translation -->
我们的评估展示了用QtD连接任务设计与关卡设计的可能性。参与者通过推测，强调了它可能帮助设计师沟通、协作的用途，并让我们很好地了解工具怎样使用、其有效性以及设计过程。不过，由于我们只测试了单个用户，没有测试团队，因此不能就它作为沟通与协作工具的可用性作出结论；这将是项目的下一步。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0061:paragraph-->
The implementation has several structural and expressive limitations. The fixed 6×6 grid, while being easy to understand, could constraint the scope and complexity of dungeons that designers can create. Participants created increasingly dense quest structures between quest 1 and 2, suggesting that the grid size may become a bottleneck for more complex narrative designs. Further, users can only select eight task types and the room generation follows similar templates for these tasks which could hinder designers’ expressivity. While participants responded positively to the Find and Gather tasks/rooms, the scope of narrative expression is still constrained compared to the full spectrum of quest possibilities in modern game design. The rigid, tile-based room generation also produces geometric layouts that, while functional, lack the organic feel that many games have.

<!-- translation -->
当前实现具有若干结构与表达方面的局限。固定6×6网格虽然容易理解，却可能限制设计师能够创建的地牢的范围与复杂性。参与者从任务1到任务2创建了越来越密集的任务结构，这表明，对于更复杂的叙事设计，网格大小可能成为瓶颈。此外，用户只能选择八种环节类型，而各类型的房间生成遵循相近模板，这可能妨碍设计师的表达。虽然参与者对寻找与采集环节及房间反应积极，但与现代游戏设计中完整的任务可能性范围相比，叙事表达仍受限制。刚性的瓦片房间生成，也产生了一种虽然可用、却缺乏许多游戏所拥有的有机感的几何布局。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0062:paragraph-->
Therefore, in future iterations of QtD we aim at increasing task-types to include more nuanced narrative elements such as npc rooms, dialogue, important events etc. as well as common quest task types that could be extracted from previous research [20, 11] as well as improving the interface for easier development such as being able to delete entire tree of task nodes or quickly show key-lock combinations. We also aim at giving more control to designers with the generated rooms such as providing more granular control over room generation parameters while maintaining the tool’s accessibility. We aim at using color-coding and quest structure terms such as satellites and kernels [4] to help structure linear quests and alternative paths.

<!-- translation -->
因此，在QtD的未来迭代中，我们希望增加环节类型，纳入更细腻的叙事要素，例如NPC房间、对话、重要事件等，也纳入可以从既有研究[20, 11]提取的常见任务环节类型；同时改进界面，让开发更容易，例如能够删除整棵环节节点树，或快速显示钥匙—锁组合。我们还希望在维持工具易用性的同时，让设计师对生成房间拥有更多控制，例如更细粒度地控制房间生成参数。我们计划利用颜色编码，以及“卫星”和“核心”等任务结构术语[4]，帮助组织线性任务与替代路径。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0063:heading-->
## 7. Conclusions

<!-- translation -->
## 7. 结论
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:QD0064:paragraph-->
This paper presented Quest to Dungeon, a tool that connects human-authored quest design with procedural generation of levels constrained by the designed quests. Our overarching goal is to address collaboration challenges at design-phase (e.g., communication between departments and design specialties, brainstorming and pre-production development) and deployment-phase (e.g., misaligned content, combination of human-authored content with PCG tools). Our tool enables designers to define quest structures through a node grid that directly impacts the procedural generation of a dungeon layout and through this, we have demonstrated how narrative-driven level design can be achieved by translating quest tasks into interconnected rooms with task-specific properties. Our evaluation emphasized the easy-to-use and effectiveness of QtD to represent quests and tasks, as well as their level and room representation. By coupling quest logic with procedural generation, the tool demonstrates how quest design can guide PCG to create dynamic, task-aligned dungeons while also making it easier for developers to iterate rapidly. While limitations exist in the current implementation, the positive reception from participants suggests that tools such as QtD approach could meaningfully contribute to more efficient and cohesive game development workflows.

<!-- translation -->
本文介绍了Quest to Dungeon，一种把人工编写的任务设计，与受这些任务约束的程序化关卡生成连接起来的工具。我们的总体目标，是应对设计阶段的协作挑战，例如部门与设计专长之间的沟通、头脑风暴和前期制作开发，以及部署阶段的协作挑战，例如内容失配、人工制作内容与PCG工具的结合。工具让设计师通过节点网格定义任务结构，直接影响地牢布局的程序化生成；我们由此展示了，如何把任务环节转化为相互连接、具有环节专属属性的房间，实现叙事驱动的关卡设计。评估强调了QtD在表示任务及其环节，以及相应关卡与房间方面的易用性和有效性。通过把任务逻辑与程序化生成耦合，工具展示了任务设计怎样引导PCG创建动态、与环节对齐的地牢，同时让开发者更容易快速迭代。虽然当前实现存在局限，参与者的积极反应表明，QtD这类工具方法，可能有意义地推动更高效、更连贯的游戏开发工作流。
<!-- endpair -->
