# 简介

棋力强大的游戏智能体，未必能让人理解它为何在某处落子。这篇论文从知识如何表示入手，用遗传编程寻找描述棋盘单元及其空间关系的短程序，再用决策树把这些局部特征组织成通向行动的路径。读者因而可以看到：某次决策经过了哪些检查，哪些特征成立，哪些不成立。

井字棋案例中，作者收集100局对弈的319个状态—行动对，从1000个特征程序中筛出21个，并展示两条决策路径；程序的自然语言说明由GPT-4o生成。四子棋案例得到439对数据，筛选后仍有379个程序，树与路径大到难以理解。两个案例共同说明了这种解释结构的用途，以及单个特征只关注少数格子时面临的规模问题。

文章把基于模式的表示和知识层级视为接近人类认知的重要方向，同时讨论它们作为必要条件与充分条件的区别。后续工作指向更丰富的特征、更高层的棋盘操作，以及把游戏规则纳入语言。

<!-- pair:en:GP0013:abstract-->
Abstract Recent advances in board game agents have led to superhuman performances in various games, but their decision-making processes remain opaque even to experienced players and are not cognitive-plausible from a human perspective. Based on criteria for cognitive-plausible systems, we introduce an approach that emphasizes interpretable knowledge representation through pattern-based and hierarchical structures. Our approach uses Genetic Programming to learn feature programs that capture spatial relationships on the game board. In addition, it utilizes decision trees to model agent behavior based on these feature programs. The result is a hierarchical, pattern-based model for generating post-hoc explanations for board game agents. We show that our method generates comprehensible explanations in Tic-Tac-Toe. We then discuss the current limitations in generalizing the approach to more complex games using Connect 4 as an example.

<!-- translation -->
摘要。棋盘游戏智能体近年的进展，使它们在多种游戏中取得了超越人类的表现；然而，即使对于经验丰富的玩家，它们的决策过程仍然不透明，从人的角度看也不具有认知合理性。依据符合认知合理性的系统应满足的标准，我们提出一种方法，着重通过基于模式和层级的结构，实现可解释的知识表示。该方法用遗传编程学习能够捕捉棋盘空间关系的特征程序，并基于这些特征程序，用决策树建模智能体行为。由此得到一种具有层级、基于模式的模型，为棋盘游戏智能体生成事后解释。我们展示该方法能够在井字棋中生成可理解的解释，随后以四子棋为例，讨论当前将其推广到更复杂游戏时存在的局限。

## 1. Introduction

<!-- translation -->
## 1 引言
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0016:paragraph-->
Although board game agents have demonstrated impressive superhuman playing strength in various domains [1, 2], they are not fully comprehensible in terms of why an action is taken, even for professional players. Furthermore, from a cognitive science perspective, the decision-making process of these agents does not resemble the way humans play games [3, 4]. Mándziuk proposes multiple “facets of cognitive-plausible playing systems” about knowledge acquisition and representation [4]. The method we propose addresses both postulates from Mańdziuk [4] concerning knowledge representation: (P1) “Game-related concepts may be effectively represented and processed with the use of pattern-based representation.” (P2) “game-related knowledge should be represented in a hierarchichal structure with various interand intra-level connections.” To that end, we present an approach for explaining board game agents by learning feature programs, i.e., programs that represent single or multiple grid cells and their relation to other cells with Genetic Programming. This is similar to P1 and the work about visual routines – a method to extract spatial information from scenes [5]. In addition, feature programs can represent intra-level connections of P2. By learning a decision tree on the feature activation of these programs, our method can highlight activated features for given state-action pairs, i.e., a hierarchical structure representing the inter-level connections of P2.

<!-- translation -->
尽管棋盘游戏智能体已在多个领域表现出超越人类的棋力[1,2]，但它们为何采取某项行动，即使专业玩家也无法充分理解。此外，从认知科学角度看，这些智能体的决策过程并不像人类下棋的方式[3,4]。Mándziuk针对知识获取与表示，提出了“符合认知合理性的游戏系统”的多个方面[4]。我们的方法回应了Mańdziuk关于知识表示的两条设想[4]：（P1）“借助基于模式的表示，可以有效地表示和处理与游戏有关的概念。”（P2）“与游戏有关的知识，应表示为一种具有多种层间及层内连接的层级结构。”为此，我们提出通过遗传编程学习特征程序，以解释棋盘游戏智能体；这里的程序表示一个或多个网格单元，以及它们与其他单元的关系。这类似于P1，也类似于视觉例程研究——从场景中提取空间信息的一种方法[5]。特征程序还可以表示P2中的层内连接。通过根据这些程序的特征激活情况学习一棵决策树，我们能够针对给定的状态—行动对，突出显示已激活的特征；这一层级结构表示了P2中的层间连接。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0017:paragraph-->
An important point worth discussing in advance is whether these postulates are necessary or sufficient for human comprehensibility and cognitive-plausibility. While pattern-based and hierarchical representations may be necessary because they correspond to human perception of recurring structures and the organization of knowledge, they are unlikely to be sufficient [6]. Human comprehensibility also depends on other aspects, such as the abstraction and combination of strategies [7], analogical thinking [8], and the ability to convey explanations in intuitive terms. We will show in our approach that adherence to the postulates contributes to more interpretable models, but a broader spectrum of mechanisms is needed to better approximate cognitive-plausibility [9].

<!-- translation -->
有一点应当事先讨论：这些设想对于人的可理解性和认知合理性，究竟是必要条件还是充分条件？基于模式与层级的表示，或许是必要的，因为它们对应人类对重复结构的感知以及知识的组织方式；但它们不太可能是充分的[6]。人的可理解性还取决于其他方面，例如策略的抽象和组合[7]、类比思维[8]，以及用直观语言传达解释的能力。我们将展示，遵循这些设想有助于得到更可解释的模型，但若要更接近认知合理性，还需要更广泛的一组机制[9]。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0024:heading-->
## 2. Related Work

<!-- translation -->
## 2 相关工作
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0025:paragraph-->
The majority of research that uses GP in the context of board games, search for heuristics for evaluating game states. These heuristics are evolved over mathematical functions for games such as Lose Checkers, Reversi, or Seven Wonders [10, 11, 12]. However, searching for heuristics is undesired, as a search algorithm is still necessary. Besides, using heuristics in a search algorithm is not fully explainable, and, in most cases, is cognitively implausible. Nevertheless, heuristics for evaluating game states, are more interpretable than using a combination of deep learning and search algorithms, similar to AlphaZero [1].

<!-- translation -->
在棋盘游戏中使用遗传编程（GP）的研究，多数是在寻找评估游戏状态的启发式函数。这些启发式函数通过对数学函数进行进化而得到，用于求败跳棋、黑白棋或《七大奇迹》等游戏[10,11,12]。然而，寻找启发式函数并不理想，因为仍然需要搜索算法。此外，在搜索算法中使用启发式函数，并不能得到完全的解释，而且大多不符合认知合理性。不过，与AlphaZero[1]那样结合深度学习与搜索算法的方式相比，评估游戏状态的启发式函数仍更容易理解。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0026:paragraph-->
Silver et al. learn logical programs which are extracted from decision trees for simple, game-related tasks [13]. In [14], an inductive logic programming algorithm is introduced that finds comprehensible game strategies by providing the game rules as building blocks for logic programs for simple board games. Currently, our method does not use game rules, as they can be derived from the state-action pairs. However, our method is more difficult to learn without game rules.

<!-- translation -->
Silver等人为简单的游戏相关任务，学习从决策树提取的逻辑程序[13]。文献[14]提出一种归纳逻辑编程算法，把游戏规则作为逻辑程序的构建模块，从而为简单棋盘游戏找到可理解的策略。我们的方法目前不使用游戏规则，因为可以从状态—行动对中推导它们；然而，没有游戏规则，我们的方法学习起来也更困难。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0027:paragraph-->
The use of programs for explainability in the context of game AI was introduced for a maze-runner agent and two simplified Atari games: Space Invaders and Asterix [15]. In contrast to generating explanations, large language models are used to directly evolve programs as policies for a wide range of digital games, as well as programs as heuristic functions for board games [16].

<!-- translation -->
游戏AI中的程序解释方法，最初用于一个迷宫奔跑智能体，以及两个简化的Atari游戏：《太空侵略者》和Asterix[15]。与生成解释不同，另一项工作用大语言模型直接进化程序：既把程序作为多种数字游戏的策略，也把程序作为棋盘游戏的启发式函数[16]。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0028:paragraph-->
The closest work to ours is by Soemers et al. [17], in which game tactics are extracted from linear models trained with self-play and converted into decision and regression trees. To be able to compare game traces collected from human participants with those collected by black-box agents in future work, we decided to generate post-hoc explanations from game traces without using the logits of trained policies, i.e., the raw output predictions of trained machine learning models before turning them into probabilities with the softmax function. Therefore, we only use state-action pairs collected from game agents without logits of a trained oracle.

<!-- translation -->
与我们最接近的是Soemers等人的工作[17]：从通过自我对弈训练的线性模型中提取游戏战术，再转成决策树和回归树。为了在未来比较人类参与者的游戏轨迹与黑箱智能体的轨迹，我们决定直接从游戏轨迹生成事后解释，而不使用已训练策略的logits，即机器学习模型通过softmax函数转为概率之前的原始预测输出。因此，我们只使用从游戏智能体收集的状态—行动对，不使用训练好的预言机模型的logits。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0029:heading-->
## 3. Program, Domain-specific Language & Problem Domain

<!-- translation -->
## 3 程序、领域专用语言与问题域
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0030:paragraph-->
Programs are represented by a typed domain-specific language (DSL) which closely resembles the Lisp programming language [18]. The search space is constructed by using a uniformly distributed probabilistic grammar [19] shown in Table 1. Our DSL is inspired by the work about visual routines [5, 13], which uses a fixed set of basic operations to assemble different perception functions to reason about properties of shapes and spatial relations. Since our focus is on board games with a grid-like board and game pieces, the building blocks of our DSL consist of control flow production rules (if clauses and Boolean operators), integers, and methods to get pieces on the board and compare them. The primitive get-board-feature(board, stateObject, object) -> feature, gets one or multiple cells and checks if they are of the same object as the third parameter. If they are the same the feature program is activated. stateObject are always cells of the current state of the board game, while object is always a type provided by the game or environment. The board feature is later able to check if for a specific state this feature program is activated, so board features are actually independent from games and board states and, thus, transferable to other games in the future.

<!-- translation -->
程序通过一种带类型的领域专用语言（DSL）表示，其形式非常接近Lisp编程语言[18]。搜索空间通过表1所示的均匀分布概率文法构建[19]。我们的DSL受到视觉例程研究[5,13]启发：用固定的一组基本操作，组合出不同的知觉函数，推理形状属性与空间关系。我们关注具有网格状棋盘和棋子的棋盘游戏，因此DSL的构建模块包括控制流产生规则（if条件与布尔运算符）、整数，以及获取和比较棋盘上棋子的方法。原语get-board-feature(board, stateObject, object) -> feature获取一个或多个单元，并检查它们是否与第三个参数属于相同对象；若相同，特征程序就被激活。stateObject始终是棋盘游戏当前状态中的单元，而object始终是游戏或环境提供的一种类型。棋盘特征随后可以检查：对于某个具体状态，这一特征程序是否被激活。因此，棋盘特征实际上独立于游戏和棋盘状态，将来可以迁移到其他游戏。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0040:heading-->
### 4.1. Overview

<!-- translation -->
### 4.1 概览
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0041:paragraph-->
The goal of this work is to generate post-hoc explanations for black-box board game agents. Figure 1 shows an overview of the proposed approach. First, we collect gameplay data by using an agent that plays in an environment, here it is Tic-Tac-Toe or Connect 4. The collected state-action pairs are then used to learn explanations with genetic programming in combination with a decision tree to select important features. Once the important features have been learned, the state-action pairs can be used to generate post-hoc explanations of the data by highlighting the activated features. Since the decision tree grows too large, we will only show selected paths in order to describe the results in Table 2. In the following, we will explain the different parts of the method.

<!-- translation -->
本研究旨在为黑箱棋盘游戏智能体生成事后解释。图1概览所提方法。首先，让智能体在环境中游玩，收集游戏过程数据；这里的环境是井字棋或四子棋。随后，用收集到的状态—行动对，结合遗传编程与决策树学习解释，并选择重要特征。学到重要特征后，可以突出显示已激活的特征，为数据生成事后解释。由于决策树过于庞大，我们只展示选定路径，以说明表2结果。下文将解释该方法的不同组成部分。

### 4.2. Deterministic Agent & Data Collection

<!-- translation -->
### 4.2 确定性智能体与数据收集
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0043:paragraph-->
To ensure a better validation, we restricted our method to deterministic minimax agents, since decisionmaking is not based on stochasticity, and is thus easier to interpret compared to MCTS agents or neural networks. It is also possible to use the agent to validate the methodology with deterministic data since the same action is always taken for a given state.

<!-- translation -->
为便于验证，我们把方法限制在确定性的极小化极大（minimax）智能体上：它的决策不基于随机性，因此比蒙特卡洛树搜索（MCTS）智能体或神经网络更容易解释。由于同一给定状态总会采取同一行动，也可以利用这种确定性数据来验证方法。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0044:heading-->
### 4.3. Genetic Programming for Learning Features

<!-- translation -->
### 4.3 用遗传编程学习特征
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0045:paragraph-->
The implementation of our tree-based genetic programming algorithm is based on [20, 21]. To initialize the population, we randomly select trees and specify their return type. Then, we recursively sample their subtrees for each parameter. To perform a mutation, we randomly select a node and a tree and sample a new subtree for the node’s return type. A one-point crossover is implemented, merging one tree with a random sub-tree of another tree that has the same return type. Tournament selection is used to select two candidates for crossover or one candidate for mutation from the population. For more details on the genetic programming algorithm, please refer to [20].

<!-- translation -->
我们基于树的遗传编程算法，依据文献[20,21]实现。初始化种群时，先随机选择树并指定其返回类型，再为各参数递归采样子树。变异时，随机选择一个节点和一棵树，再按照该节点的返回类型，采样一棵新的子树。我们实现了单点交叉：把一棵树与另一棵树中随机选取、返回类型相同的子树合并。算法采用锦标赛选择，从种群中选择两个交叉候选，或一个变异候选。更多遗传编程算法细节见[20]。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0046:paragraph-->
The fitness function maximizes the number of times features appear in the set of provided states 𝑆:

<!-- translation -->
适应度函数使特征在给定状态集合S中出现的次数最大化：
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0047:equation-->
$$\operatorname{fitness}=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sum_{s\in S}\rho(s)}{|S|},&\text{if }\rho\notin D_F,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}\qquad(1)$$

<!-- translation -->
$$\operatorname{fitness}=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sum_{s\in S}\rho(s)}{|S|},&\rho\notin D_F\text{时},\\0,&\text{其他情况}.\end{cases}\qquad(1)$$
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0048:paragraph-->
where ρ is the currently evolved feature program, which returns 1 if the feature is activated and 0 if the feature is not activated. We also use a regularizer to prevent the trees from growing too large. We implement a feature program database D_F that stores the features with the highest fitness every t = 50 generations for a total of g generations. This results in |D_F| = g/t feature programs in D_F.

<!-- translation -->
其中，ρ是当前进化的特征程序：特征激活时返回1，未激活时返回0。我们还使用一个正则化项，防止树长得过大。我们实现了一个特征程序数据库D_F，在总共g代的进化过程中，每隔t=50代保存适应度最高的特征。由此，D_F中会有|D_F|=g/t个特征程序。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0049:algorithm_caption-->
Algorithm 1 Overview of the whole approach.

<!-- translation -->
算法1. 整体方法概览。

<!-- pair:en:GP0051:paragraph-->
If an evolved feature program is already present in the feature database, then we always return 0 as the fitness. This ensures that these features will most likely not be selected for further evolution. We evolve the same population after saving the best features in the feature database so that we do not have to re-initialize the population. This allows the GP algorithm to run like an open-ended evolution, adding new features throughout all iterations. This is also possible thanks to the probabilistic grammar, which defines programs through recursion.

<!-- translation -->
如果进化出的特征程序已存在于特征数据库中，我们就始终把适应度设为0。这能确保这些特征很可能不会被选中继续进化。把最佳特征保存进数据库后，我们仍继续进化同一种群，无需重新初始化。因此，GP算法可以像开放式进化那样运行，在各轮迭代中不断加入新特征。概率文法通过递归定义程序，也使这一点成为可能。
<!-- endpair -->

A drawback of counting how often features occur in the states is that programs such as (get-board-feature $1 (get $1 (cell 1 0)) (get-game-piece (get $1 (cell 1 0)))) have maximum fitness because they compare the same cell. <!-- translation -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0053:heading-->
### 4.4. From Features To Explanations

<!-- translation -->
### 4.4 从特征到解释
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0054:paragraph-->
After collecting features for a predetermined number of generations t, we run all feature programs D_F on the given states. Next, we train a decision tree T_{D_F} using a standard stochastic greedy decision tree learner [22] that maps the output of the feature programs to the corresponding actions Y_a for each state in S. This decision tree represents the hierarchical structure of how features are connected. When we calculate the feature importance φ, i.e., the Gini importance, it becomes clear that only a small subset of the found features D̃_F ⊆ D_F is actually used to map the activated features to the actions. Therefore, when training the decision tree, we filter the features and keep only those with an importance of φ > 0. We train a second decision tree T_{D̃_F} on the subset of D̃_F, to have a concise mapping of the nodes in the decision tree and the programs in D̃_F, since only the best subset D̃_F of previously found programs is used.

<!-- translation -->
经过预定的t代收集特征后，我们在给定状态上运行D_F中的所有特征程序。接着，用标准的随机贪心决策树学习器[22]训练决策树T_{D_F}，将特征程序的输出，映射到S中各状态所对应的行动Y_a。这棵树表示特征之间如何连接的层级结构。计算特征重要性φ，即基尼重要性时，可以看出：找到的特征中，实际用于把激活特征映射到行动的，只是一个很小的子集D̃_F ⊆ D_F。因此，在训练决策树时，我们筛选特征，只保留重要性φ>0的特征。随后，在子集D̃_F上训练第二棵决策树T_{D̃_F}；由于只使用此前找到的程序中的最佳子集D̃_F，决策树节点与这些程序之间的映射更加简洁。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0055:paragraph-->
We use the collected states as input to create post-hoc explanations and traverse the decision paths ρ̃, with ρ̃ ⊆ D̃_F of the decision tree to make the activated or non-activated feature programs visible. The pseudo code of this procedure is given in Algorithm 1.

<!-- translation -->
我们以收集到的状态为输入，生成事后解释，并遍历决策树的决策路径ρ̃（ρ̃ ⊆ D̃_F），使已激活或未激活的特征程序显现出来。该过程的伪代码见算法1。

<!-- pair:en:GP0061:heading-->
### 5.1. Case Study: Tic-Tac-Toe Explanations

<!-- translation -->
### 5.1 案例研究：井字棋解释
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0062:paragraph-->
To demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, we selected Tic-Tac-Toe as the test environment because the simple game logic enables us to easily interpret the actions chosen by the minimax agent. When two minimax agents play against each other, the game always ends in a tie. Therefore, we collected data by having a random agent play against the minimax agent. In total, both agents played 100 games, which resulted in 319 state-action pairs. We only collected data after the minimax agent made a move with the X symbol because we only wanted to explain the minimax agent’s decisions.

<!-- translation -->
为展示所提方法的有效性，我们选择井字棋作为测试环境；简单的游戏逻辑，使我们容易理解极小化极大智能体选择的行动。两个极小化极大智能体对弈时，总会以平局结束。因此，我们让一个随机智能体与极小化极大智能体对弈，以收集数据。二者共进行100局，得到319个状态—行动对。由于只想解释极小化极大智能体的决策，我们只在它用X落子后收集数据。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0063:paragraph-->
Next, we ran the genetic programming (GP) algorithm on the states for g = 50,000 generations, maintaining a population size of 200 and a tournament selection size of 20. Every t = 50 generations, the best feature program in the population was saved in the feature database. After the GP algorithm is completed, there are a total of |D_F| = 1000 feature programs in the database. After filtering the features by the calculated feature importance score of the decision tree, only |D̃_F| = 21 feature programs remain for explaining the decision-making process. Table 2 shows examples of the reasoning process, and provides an explanation of the programs in natural language, as well as a short concise description of the reasoning process in the caption.

<!-- translation -->
接着，我们在这些状态上运行遗传编程（GP）算法，共g=50,000代，种群规模为200，锦标赛选择规模为20。每隔t=50代，把种群中最佳特征程序保存进特征数据库。GP算法结束后，数据库中共有|D_F|=1000个特征程序。依据决策树计算出的特征重要性分数进行筛选后，只剩|D̃_F|=21个特征程序用于解释决策过程。表2展示了推理过程示例，提供程序的自然语言解释，并在表题中简要说明推理过程。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0064:heading-->
### 5.2. Case Study: Connect 4

<!-- translation -->
### 5.2 案例研究：四子棋
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0065:paragraph-->
We also ran experiments on the game Connect 4. The data collection process is similar to that of Tic-Tac-Toe. However, we do not let a minimax agent play against a random agent, as most games end after the minimax agent has played four pieces. We let two minimax agents with different search depths play against each other in order to collect data which resulted in 439 state-action pairs. For the GP algorithm, we used g = 100,000 generations, maintaining the same population and tournament size throughout. This resulted in |D_F| = 2000 feature programs. After training the decision tree and filtering the programs by Gini importance, only |D̃_F| = 379 remained. We created post hoc explanations for a few example states, but the decision tree and path were too large and incomprehensible. Upon examining the important features, we noticed that many of them only considered one or two cells, which was sufficient for Tic-Tac-Toe since the game’s complexity is much smaller. We identified multiple key issues that need improvement for learning features with genetic programming:

<!-- translation -->
我们也在四子棋上开展实验。数据收集过程类似于井字棋，但没有让极小化极大智能体与随机智能体对弈，因为多数对局会在极小化极大智能体落下四枚棋子后结束。为收集数据，我们让两个搜索深度不同的极小化极大智能体对弈，得到439个状态—行动对。GP算法运行g=100,000代，保持相同的种群规模与锦标赛选择规模，得到|D_F|=2000个特征程序。训练决策树并依据基尼重要性筛选程序后，只剩|D̃_F|=379个。我们为少量示例状态生成了事后解释，但决策树及路径过于庞大，难以理解。检查重要特征后发现，其中很多只考虑一两个单元；井字棋的复杂性低得多，这样做尚且足够。我们识别出用遗传编程学习特征时，需要改进的几个关键问题：
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0066:list_item-->
• Currently, the fitness function only counts how often a feature appears in states. To reduce the number of necessary features, the number of cells the feature looks at should also be maximized.

<!-- translation -->
• 当前适应度函数只统计特征在状态中出现的频率。为减少所需特征数量，还应使每个特征考察的单元数量最大化。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0067:list_item-->
• The DSL should be adapted to include more high-level functions that are also plausible from a cognitive science viewpoint, such as counting pieces.

<!-- translation -->
• 应调整DSL，纳入更多从认知科学角度看也合理的高层函数，例如棋子计数。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0068:list_item-->
• Including the game rules in the DSL, could also lead to better features and runtime.

<!-- translation -->
• 将游戏规则纳入DSL，也可能改善特征和运行时间。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0069:heading-->
## 6. Conclusion

<!-- translation -->
## 6 结论
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:GP0070:paragraph-->
In this work, we introduced an approach and showed preliminary results for creating cognitive-plausible explanations of board game agents. Our approach is based on visual routines [5] and has a hierarchical, pattern-based structure that resembles a more human-intuitive way of playing games [3, 4], which we also discussed for sufficiency and necessity. We showed the branching of a decision path for explanations of two collected state-action pairs in the game Tic-Tac-Toe and outlined how to scale the approach to more complex games.

<!-- translation -->
本研究提出了一种方法，并展示了为棋盘游戏智能体创建符合认知合理性的解释的初步结果。该方法基于视觉例程[5]，具有层级、基于模式的结构，接近更符合人类直觉的游戏游玩方式[3,4]；我们也讨论了这一结构的充分性与必要性。我们展示了井字棋中两个已收集状态—行动对的解释所对应的决策路径分支，并勾勒了将方法扩展到更复杂游戏的方向。
<!-- endpair -->
