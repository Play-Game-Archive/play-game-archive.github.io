# 简介

“游戏机制”常被用来解释游戏怎样运作，却未必具有足够明确的分析边界。本文从游戏本体论、对象与行动、玩家意图及设计语汇等问题出发，逐项检视这个术语承担的不同任务，主张以更清楚的描述框架处理游戏结构与游玩活动。论文发表于FDG 2026，原文与中文全文对照，保留作者机构、摘要、正文、致谢及全部39条参考文献。

## Abstract

<!-- translation -->
## 摘要
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0008:paragraph-->
“Game mechanics” is a frequently referenced yet contested term in game studies. There have been some efforts to define it, of which perhaps the most widely known is Sicart’s definition of game mechanics as “methods invoked by agents interacting with the game world.” The term has been explored variously for its tangled relationship to game rules, game narrative, or the player’s ability to complete game tasks that involve a non-trivial amount of manual dexterity, among others. Despite its ubiquitous usage, the question of what exactly is a game mechanic persists. Yet rather than attempt to re-define it, this paper proposes that the term has overstayed its welcome and is not productive in the study of games in its current form. Each of the contributing authors offers a position statement in support of this proposal. The concluding discussion establishes the categorial nature of game mechanics and offers some potential solutions for moving beyond it.

<!-- translation -->
“游戏机制”是游戏研究中经常被引用却又备受争议的术语。学界曾尝试对它下定义，其中最广为人知的或许是 Sicart 的定义：游戏机制是“行动者在与游戏世界互动时调用的方法”。围绕这一术语的探讨涉及它与游戏规则、游戏叙事之间错综复杂的关系，也涉及玩家完成需要相当手部操作技巧的游戏任务的能力，等等。尽管这个词无处不在，游戏机制究竟是什么的问题仍然存在。不过，本文不打算重新定义它，而是提出：这个术语已经停留得太久，以它目前的形式，已无法对游戏研究发挥有益作用。每位参与撰写的作者都提出一份支持这一主张的立场陈述。最后的讨论确立游戏机制作为范畴性用语的性质，并提出若干超越这一术语的可能途径。
<!-- endpair -->

## 1 Introduction

<!-- translation -->
## 1 引言
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0016:paragraph-->
In the world of games, the term “game mechanics” enjoys widespread recognition. It is used by popular online gaming magazines in their critical reviews, by game design practitioners to generate discussion about new ideas, and by game scholars for focused analysis. Yet despite finding purchase in these various circles, the term has resisted definition. While being a mainstay in the background of game discourse, when brought into the foreground it wrinkles brows in confusion. Press for meaning and it vanishes. For the study of games, this is particularly concerning, since arguments are constructed off of the back of clearly defined terminology. Therefore, it is important to ask the question: what does “game mechanics” actually mean?

<!-- translation -->
在游戏世界里，“游戏机制”一词广为人知。大众网络游戏杂志在评论中使用它，游戏设计从业者用它引发对新构想的讨论，游戏学者则用它开展有针对性的分析。然而，尽管它已在这些不同圈子里立足，却始终难以下定义。它一直是游戏话语背景中的常客，一旦被推到前景，反而让人困惑地皱起眉头；追问其意义，它便消失不见。这一点尤其令游戏研究担忧，因为论证是建立在定义清晰的术语之上的。因此，必须提出这个问题：“游戏机制”究竟意味着什么？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0017:paragraph-->
Quite simply, this paper proposes that game mechanics does not mean much of anything. More than that, this paper contends that it cannot mean anything more than it does at the moment, a vague gesture at something like a game rule. This is due to the fact that any attempt to circumscribe the common usage of the term results in an idiosyncratic definition that does not fully capture the breadth of the original meaning. In other words, the original meaning is too broad to be fully captured. Therefore when in the background of the discussion it can be effective as shorthand for some part of the game, but when brought into focus, it fails to provide the foundations needed for analysis. Yet why is this the case? What makes game mechanics indefinable?

<!-- translation -->
简单地说，本文提出，游戏机制几乎没有什么明确意义。不仅如此，本文认为，它所能具有的意义也不可能比目前更多：无非是模糊地指向某种类似游戏规则的东西。这是因为，任何试图圈定该词通常用法的尝试，最终都会得到一个特立独行的定义，无法充分涵盖原有意义的广度。换言之，其原有意义过于宽泛，无法被完整把握。所以，当它处于讨论背景中时，可以有效地充当游戏某一部分的简称；但一旦成为焦点，便不能提供分析所需的基础。然而，为什么会这样？是什么让游戏机制无法定义？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0018:paragraph-->
To answer this question, each of the authors of this paper offers his own position statement on game mechanics. Jussi Holopainen discusses the term in relation to both the pedagogy of teaching mechanics and the problems of its application to the study of a commonplace “mechanic”, lockpicking. Espen Aarseth investigates the origins of mechanics, reflecting on how it became so popular in the first place through a detailed dissection of Hunicke et al.’s [14] framework. Pawel Grabarczyk problematizes the deployment of the term, highlighting just how often it becomes empty of meaning before concluding that mechanics is a type of categorial term. Leon Xiao considers its lack of impact from a legalistic perspective and how mechanics neither adds nor detracts from the reports. Finally, Benjamin Horn reflects on the theoretical baggage that comes with the task of constructing a new definition, and the benefits of starting afresh.

<!-- translation -->
为回答这个问题，本文每位作者各自提出一份关于游戏机制的立场陈述。Jussi Holopainen 从机制教学，以及把这一术语用于研究一种常见“机制”——开锁——时出现的问题两个方面讨论它。Espen Aarseth 通过详细剖析 Hunicke 等人的框架 [14]，考察机制概念的起源，反思它最初如何如此流行。Pawel Grabarczyk 质疑这一术语的使用方式，指出它何其频繁地变得空无意义，继而得出结论：机制是一类范畴性用语。Leon Xiao 从法律视角考察它为何没有实质影响，以及机制一词如何既未给报告增加什么，也未使报告损失什么。最后，Benjamin Horn 反思建立新定义这一任务附带的理论包袱，以及另起炉灶的好处。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0019:paragraph-->
The consequence of these disparate but shared positions is that the current paradigm for the usage of game mechanics is no longer tenable. The term has an unbearable vacuity to it that cannot be avoided. Therefore, it is the conclusion of the authors that while game scholars by all means may continue to use the term as productive shorthand, they should also consider looking for more systematic alternatives.

<!-- translation -->
这些各有差异却又相通的立场共同意味着，当前使用游戏机制的范式已无法维持。这个术语具有一种令人难以忍受、又无法回避的空泛。因此，作者们得出的结论是：游戏学者当然可以继续把它用作有益的简称，但也应考虑寻找更系统的替代方案。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0020:heading-->
## 2 Literature Review

<!-- translation -->
## 2 文献回顾
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0021:paragraph-->
Even though game mechanics has resisted definition, it has not been for want of trying. Björk, Lundgren, and Holopainen [3] identify that “various communities of gamers and game designers…used the concept of mechanics” but that its usage was “general” and “not useful for academic research.” They then offer that “the concept of mechanics, i.e. that a game can be regarded as an entity put together by a number of smaller components, seems to be useful”, which gives them the justification for their structuring and classification of these components as “game design patterns.” Mechanics as presented here seems to refer to components of interaction in games.

<!-- translation -->
游戏机制一直难以下定义，却并非因为无人尝试。Björk、Lundgren 和 Holopainen [3] 指出，“不同的玩家和游戏设计者社群……使用了机制这一概念”，但这种用法“笼统”，而且“对学术研究没有帮助”。他们接着提出，“机制这一概念，也就是可以把游戏视为由若干较小组成部分拼合而成的实体，似乎是有用的”，并以此说明将这些组成部分组织、分类为“游戏设计模式”的合理性。这里呈现的机制，似乎指游戏中互动的组成部分。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0022:paragraph-->
The idea of mechanics as components is taken up by Hunicke, LeBlanc, and Zubek [14] as a part of the influential MDA framework. MDA stands for Mechanics, Dynamics, and Aesthetics, and it is interesting to note that neither dynamics nor aesthetics stood the test of time. They initially define mechanics - similar to Björk, Lundgren, and Holopainen - as “the particular components of the game, at the level of data representation and algorithms” (p. 2). It should be noted that the second part of this definition appears to add a programmatic understanding of mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
Hunicke、LeBlanc 和 Zubek [14] 在颇具影响力的 MDA 框架中接续了把机制理解为组成部分的想法。MDA 代表机制（Mechanics）、动态（Dynamics）和美感（Aesthetics）；值得注意的是，动态与美感这两个概念都没有经受住时间的考验。他们最初的机制定义与 Björk、Lundgren 和 Holopainen 相似，是“游戏的具体组成部分，位于数据表征和算法的层次”（第 2 页）。应当注意，这一定义的后半部分似乎为机制增添了一种程序层面的理解。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0023:paragraph-->
This programmatic aspect of their definition disappears in their second, somewhat more detailed attempt with the same framework: “Mechanics are the various actions, behaviours, and control mechanisms afforded to the player within a game context” (p. 3). They give some examples: “shuffling, trick-taking and betting” are “the mechanics of card games” (p. 4). Yet even so, their second definition is arguably still too broad for academic use. For instance, would a player throwing their cards on the ground in anger be considered a game mechanic? It is certainly an action afforded by the cards, and a player might do so in the context of the game (i.e. she may be losing and therefore angry). Could storming away from a game in anger be a component of a game?

<!-- translation -->
在同一框架里，他们第二次、也稍为详细的尝试中，这种程序层面的内容消失了：“机制是游戏情境中向玩家提供的各种行动、行为与控制机制”（第 3 页）。他们举例说，“洗牌、吃墩与下注”就是“纸牌游戏的机制”（第 4 页）。尽管如此，第二个定义对于学术使用而言仍可说过于宽泛。例如，玩家愤怒地把牌扔到地上，算不算游戏机制？这当然是纸牌所允许的一种行动，玩家也可能在游戏情境中这样做——例如，她可能因为快输了而生气。愤然离开游戏，会是游戏的一个组成部分吗？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0024:paragraph-->
Rouse [31] - in his book on game design - writes that game mechanics are “what the players are able to do in the game-world, how they do it, and how that leads to a compelling design gameplay experience” (p. 310). The game mechanics section of a design document for a game is, in Rouse’s opinion, “the most important part” (p. 361). He states that the “Game Mechanics section could also be described as the ‘gameplay’ section, since it describes what the players are allowed to do in the game and how the game is played” (Ibid.). It is interesting that the term used for the most important section of a game design document could be replaced one-for-one with another

<!-- translation -->
Rouse [31] 在游戏设计著作中写道，游戏机制是“玩家在游戏世界中能够做什么，如何去做，以及这些如何导向一种引人入胜的设计与游戏体验”（第 310 页）。Rouse 认为，游戏设计文档的游戏机制部分是“最重要的部分”（第 361 页）。他说，“游戏机制部分也可以叫作‘玩法’部分，因为它描述玩家被允许在游戏中做什么，以及游戏如何进行”（同上）。耐人寻味的是，用于游戏设计文档最重要部分的这个术语，竟可以被另一个词一对一替换。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0025:paragraph-->
These early definitions of game mechanics are mostly confined to discussions of game design, but Järvinen [17] more rigorously investigates the term as applied to the study of games. Specifically, he claims in his doctoral thesis that “game mechanics are means to guide the player and the game into particular behaviour by constraining the space of possible plans to attain goals” (p. 254). Implicit here is the notion that mechanics are verbs - things that players do. To give an example of Järvinen’s definition in action: in Super Mario Bros [27] the player can move around and jump as the protagonist plumber Mario. In order to reach the end, the player must move and jump. Therefore moving and jumping are the primary mechanics of Super Mario Bros.

<!-- translation -->
这些早期游戏机制定义大多局限在游戏设计的讨论中；Järvinen [17] 则更严格地考察了这一术语如何用于游戏研究。具体来说，他在博士论文中主张，“游戏机制是通过限制达成目标的可能计划空间，引导玩家与游戏采取特定行为的手段”（第 254 页）。这里隐含着一种看法：机制是动词，是玩家做的事情。举一个运用 Järvinen 定义的例子：在《Super Mario Bros.》[27] 中，玩家扮演水管工主角 Mario，能够移动和跳跃。为了抵达终点，玩家必须移动和跳跃。因此，移动与跳跃就是《Super Mario Bros.》的主要机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0026:paragraph-->
Sicart [35] points out that Järvinen’s definition “is rather deterministic: mechanics seem to exist so that goals can be achieved, and thus there would be no mechanics if the game, or a specific set of actions, has no goals.” He gives the example of sandbox games like Sim City [24], which, by Järvinen’s understanding, would have no mechanics. With the objective of a more inclusive definition, Sicart arrives at the following: “game mechanics are methods invoked by agents, designed for interaction with the game state.” An advantage of Sicart’s definition is that it “departs from anthropocentrism of previous approaches”; the computer which is playing the game may also make use of the game’s mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
Sicart [35] 指出，Järvinen 的定义“相当具有决定论色彩：机制似乎为了达成目标而存在，因此，如果游戏或某一组具体行动没有目标，就不会有机制”。他以《Sim City》[24] 这样的沙盒游戏为例：按照 Järvinen 的理解，它将没有机制。为提出一个更具包容性的定义，Sicart 得出以下表述：“游戏机制是由行动者调用、为与游戏状态互动而设计的方法。”Sicart 这一定义的优点在于，它“脱离了先前路径的人类中心主义”：参与游戏的计算机也可以利用游戏机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0027:paragraph-->
However one of the challenges for both Järvinen and Sicart is the sticky question of rules vs. mechanics. Järvinen writes that “a game mechanic makes a particular set of rules available to the player in the form of prescribed causal relations between game elements and their consequence to particular game states” (p. 254), while Sicart believes that “Game mechanics are concerned with the actual interaction with the game state, while rules provide the possibility space where that interaction is possible, regulating as well the transition between states.” Neither of these statements clearly distinguishes between rules and mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
然而，Järvinen 和 Sicart 都面临一个难题，即规则与机制的关系。Järvinen 写道，“游戏机制以游戏元素及其对特定游戏状态产生的后果之间预先规定的因果关系为形式，让玩家能够利用某一组特定规则”（第 254 页）；Sicart 则认为，“游戏机制关涉与游戏状态的实际互动，规则提供这种互动得以发生的可能性空间，同时调节状态之间的转换”。这两种表述都没有清楚区分规则与机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0028:paragraph-->
There have been further attempts to unpack the rules vs. mechanics relationship. Aarseth and Grabarczyk [1] in their ontological meta-model note that “Rules can be understood as mechanics that the player perceives, while mechanics are embedded in the structure independently of what the user thinks of them…Rules are either interpreted mechanics, or postulated norms” (p. 14). Vargas-Iglasias and Navarrete-Cardeo [38] want to move beyond the supposed “rule-mechanic binomial” and propose that “mechanics, understood as algorithmic devices, are part of rules in that the former involve the latter.” Nevertheless, it is still difficult to draw an explicit division between the two terms.

<!-- translation -->
此后还有更多尝试，试图厘清规则与机制的关系。Aarseth 和 Grabarczyk [1] 在本体论元模型中指出，“规则可以被理解为玩家所感知的机制，而机制独立于使用者如何看待它们，嵌入结构之中……规则或者是经解释的机制，或者是假定的规范”（第 14 页）。Vargas-Iglasias 和 Navarrete-Cardeo [38] 希望超越所谓“规则—机制二元组”，提出“作为算法装置来理解的机制，是规则的一部分，因为前者涉及后者”。不过，仍然很难在这两个术语之间划出明确界线。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0029:paragraph-->
Taking a step back, the result of these various attempts to define mechanics has been a general lack of consensus on how to use the term in academic research, which can be seen in the variety of different approaches to its application. Lim et al [22] have used the term to discuss the development of serious games, Donaldson [6] explores it in regards to skilful play in League of Legends [30], Dubbelman [7, 8] has suggested it for narrative analysis, Ben-Sadoun and Alvarez [2] combine it with cognitive functions from a psychoanalytic perspective to build their “Gameplay Bricks” model, while Mayo [25] uses it for a close comparative reading of two games and their “ludic mechanics…such as game save and respawn rules.” There is no consensus in the field of game studies on how best to apply the term game mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
退后一步看，这些界定机制的不同尝试，最终并没有形成关于学术研究中应如何使用这一术语的普遍共识；其应用路径的多样性就体现了这一点。Lim 等人 [22] 用它讨论严肃游戏的开发，Donaldson [6] 探讨它与《League of Legends》[30] 熟练游玩的关系，Dubbelman [7, 8] 建议将其用于叙事分析，Ben-Sadoun 与 Alvarez [2] 从精神分析视角把它和认知功能结合起来，建立“Gameplay Bricks”模型，Mayo [25] 则用它细读比较两款游戏及其“游戏性机制……例如游戏存档与重生规则”。游戏研究领域尚未就如何最好地运用“游戏机制”一词达成共识。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0030:paragraph-->
Lo et al’s [23] survey corroborates this claim. Performing a statistical analysis of the use of the term “mechanics” in the study of games, they identified a total of 329 papers potentially relevant to the topic, which were then categorized by degree of relevance. Remarkably, they discovered “at least 49 different definitions of ‘game mechanics’ distributed across the literature” (p. 6). These definitions were noted to be similar yet all original enough to be distinctive. Commonalities were the use of the terms “rules” and “actions”, but they found it “unlikely that the set of 49 definitions is free from conflicts” (Ibid.). The authors conclude that “when we talk about game mechanics, it seems quite unlikely that we mean the same thing” (p. 9).

<!-- translation -->
Lo 等人 [23] 的调查印证了这一判断。他们对游戏研究中“机制”一词的使用进行统计分析，共找到 329 篇可能相关的论文，再按相关程度分类。令人瞩目的是，他们发现“文献中散布着至少 49 种不同的‘游戏机制’定义”（第 6 页）。这些定义相似，但又各自具有足够的独创性，可以相互区别。共同点包括使用“规则”和“行动”等术语，不过他们认为，“这组 49 个定义不包含冲突”的可能性很低（同上）。作者们得出的结论是，“当我们谈论游戏机制时，所指恰好相同的可能性似乎相当低”（第 9 页）。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0031:paragraph-->
The term game mechanics has therefore ultimately resisted definition and proven problematic by sowing confusion through its many applications. Still it has persisted while occupying nebulous theoretical territory. The position statements below offer more detailed perspectives on how to chart a course through this territory and towards a more systematic paradigm.

<!-- translation -->
因此，游戏机制这个术语终究一直难以下定义，并因各种应用不断制造混淆，显示出自身的问题。尽管如此，它仍持续存在，占据着模糊的理论地带。以下立场陈述将更详细地提出不同看法，说明如何穿越这片地带，走向更系统的范式。
<!-- endpair -->

## 3 Position Statements

<!-- translation -->
## 3 立场陈述
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0033:heading-->
### 3.1 Jussi Holopainen

<!-- translation -->
### 3.1 Jussi Holopainen
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0034:paragraph-->
Based on two decades of teaching and researching game design, I argue that the term game mechanics is too imprecise to be useful across contexts, and that more exact, context-dependent concepts provide clearer and more practical foundations for both game design education and game analysis.

<!-- translation -->
基于二十年的游戏设计教学与研究，我认为，“游戏机制”一词过于不精确，无法在不同语境中有效使用；更准确、依语境而定的概念，才能为游戏设计教育与游戏分析提供更清楚、更切实可行的基础。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0035:heading-->
### 3.1.1 Teaching Game Design: Why “Game Mechanics” Is Not Precise Enough.

<!-- translation -->
### 3.1.1 游戏设计教学：“游戏机制”为何不够精确。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0036:paragraph-->
My game design teaching experience has shown that students readily adopt the term game mechanics, but often in ways that reveal its conceptual looseness. While the term functions as convenient shorthand, its ambiguity can obscure rather than clarify the design situation, particularly for novice designers learning how to articulate intentions and evaluate their own work.

<!-- translation -->
我的游戏设计教学经验表明，学生很容易采用游戏机制这个术语，但他们的用法常常暴露出概念的松散。虽然它可以充当便利的简称，但其歧义可能遮蔽设计情境，而非澄清它；对于正在学习如何表达意图、评价自身作品的新手设计者来说，尤其如此。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0037:paragraph-->
Students use game mechanics in at least two distinct ways. First, they apply the term to concrete features in a specific game: “My jumping mechanic doesn’t feel right; I want it closer to Celeste.” In these situations, the mechanic points toward a particular behavioural and experiential phenomenon. What the student is actually engaging with, however, is not a generalizable “mechanic” but a constellation of elements: avatar control tuning, input timing, animation curves, dynamic gravity settings, camera response, and the felt rhythm of action. By treating all of these under the singular label of “mechanic”, the student designers risk flattening the design space and masking the specific design moves available to them. More precise concepts, such as movement model, control responsiveness, timing window, or feedback loops, better support the reflective practices essential to design reasoning ([34], [20], [19], [21]).

<!-- translation -->
学生至少以两种不同方式使用游戏机制。第一，他们用它指某款具体游戏中的具体特征：“我的跳跃机制感觉不对，我想让它更接近《Celeste》。”在这种情况下，“机制”指向某个特定的行为与体验现象。然而，学生实际面对的并不是一种可概括推广的“机制”，而是一组元素的组合：化身控制的调校、输入时机、动画曲线、动态重力设置、镜头响应，以及行动中感受到的节奏。把这些全都归在单一的“机制”标签之下，可能使学生设计者把设计空间压平，掩盖自己能够采取的具体设计举措。诸如运动模型、控制响应性、时机窗口或反馈循环等更精确的概念，更能支持设计推理所必需的反思实践（[34]、[20]、[19]、[21]）。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0038:paragraph-->
Second, students use game mechanics at a more abstract level to refer to design goals, intentions, or experiments: “I want floaty jump mechanics” or “I’d like to try a worker-placement mechanic here.” These uses conflate two distinct roles: articulating player experience goals and referring to established formal structures found in existing games. For beginners, collapsing these distinctions under the umbrella of “mechanic” can make it harder to understand whether they are setting an experiential target, identifying a systemic structure, or proposing a design experiment. More context-sensitive terms, such as desired movement feel, interaction pattern, resource allocation structure, or design hypothesis, help students distinguish between goals, systems, procedures, and design situations, thereby improving the precision of their design discourse. While a student might refer to a “worker-placement mechanic”, its apparent precision comes from the long-standing codification of worker placement as a specific interaction pattern within tabletop game design. In other words, the clarity derives from historical convention and a specific design context, rather than from the conceptual stability of the term as a game mechanic itself.

<!-- translation -->
第二，学生在更抽象的层次上，用游戏机制指称设计目标、意图或实验：“我想要轻飘飘的跳跃机制”，或“我想在这里试试工人放置机制”。这些用法把两种不同作用混在一起：一是表达玩家体验目标，二是指称既有游戏中已经确立的形式结构。对初学者而言，把这些区别统摄到“机制”之下，会使他们更难理解自己究竟是在设定体验目标、辨认系统结构，还是提出设计实验。期待的运动手感、互动模式、资源分配结构或设计假设等更依语境而定的术语，可以帮助学生区分目标、系统、程序与设计情境，从而提高设计话语的精确性。学生或许会说“工人放置机制”，但这一说法看似精确，是因为工人放置在桌面游戏设计中早已被规范为一种特定互动模式。换言之，清晰性来自历史惯例和具体设计语境，而非“游戏机制”本身在概念上稳定。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0039:paragraph-->
Additionally, the term “mechanic” often leads students to assume that the player must be aware of the phenomenon in question. Yet classroom discussions regularly refer to elements such as hidden resource generation, background simulation, or systemic nudges that players may never consciously notice. Referring to all of these as “mechanics” could potentially erase useful distinctions between player actions, system behaviours, and background processes (cf. [1]).

<!-- translation -->
此外，“机制”一词常常让学生以为，玩家必须意识到所讨论的现象。然而，课堂讨论经常涉及隐藏的资源生成、后台模拟，或玩家可能从未自觉注意到的系统性引导。如果把这些全称为“机制”，就可能抹掉玩家行动、系统行为与后台过程之间有用的区别（参见 [1]）。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0040:paragraph-->
For these reasons, while “game mechanic” remains a convenient entry point into design discussions, it is pedagogically insufficient as a conceptual tool. Its looseness encourages students to gloss over distinctions that are crucial for understanding how games actually function. By introducing more exact, context-dependent terms (e.g. action model, activity structure, system behaviour, feedback loop, representational strategy) educators can give students a richer vocabulary for framing problems, analysing design situations, and making deliberate design moves. Precision in terminology encourages precision in thinking, which in turn supports more intentional and reflective game design practice.

<!-- translation -->
因此，尽管“游戏机制”仍是进入设计讨论的便利入口，作为概念工具，它在教学上并不充分。它的松散性会让学生略过一些对于理解游戏实际如何运作至关重要的区别。引入更准确、更依语境而定的术语，例如行动模型、活动结构、系统行为、反馈循环、表征策略，可以让教育者为学生提供更丰富的词汇，用于界定问题、分析设计情境，并作出自觉的设计举措。术语精确能够促进思考精确，进而支持更有意识、更具反思性的游戏设计实践。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0041:heading-->
### 3.1.2 Game Analysis.

<!-- translation -->
### 3.1.2 游戏分析。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0042:paragraph-->
From an analytical perspective, the limitations of game mechanics become even more apparent. My recent collaborations on the analysis of lockpicking minigames began with the assumption, common in game studies, that “lockpicking” could be straightforwardly described as a game mechanic. This assumption quickly proved inadequate. Rather than clarifying the phenomenon under examination, the term obscured precisely the distinctions that analytic work must reveal: what kind of action is occurring, what kind of activity it represents, and how the game constructs a meaningful engagement with a real-world practice.

<!-- translation -->
从分析角度看，游戏机制的局限就更明显了。我最近参与的开锁小游戏分析，起初采取了游戏研究中常见的假定，认为“开锁”可以直接被描述为游戏机制。这一假定很快便显得不足。这个词没有澄清所考察的现象，反而恰好遮蔽了分析工作必须揭示的区别：发生了何种行动，它代表何种活动，以及游戏怎样建构对某项现实实践的有意义参与。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0043:paragraph-->
Lockpicking in games is not a single “mechanic”; it is a mediated activity shaped by gestures, constraints, sensory cues, temporal rhythms, and representational choices. Collapsing all of this complexity under a single label flattens the analytic terrain and prevents us from asking finer-grained questions. To understand how a real-world manual skill requiring precision and patience is translated, often radically reconfigured, into a playable form, we required concepts that better distinguish between player actions, system behaviours, and activity representations.

<!-- translation -->
游戏中的开锁不是单一“机制”，而是一种由手势、约束、感官线索、时间节奏与表征选择共同塑造的媒介化活动。把全部复杂性压缩成一个标签，就会压平分析地形，阻止我们提出更细致的问题。为了理解一项需要精确与耐心的现实手工技能怎样被转译、而且常常被彻底重新配置为可玩形式，我们需要更能区分玩家行动、系统行为与活动表征的概念。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0044:paragraph-->
This shift opened dialogues with several theoretical traditions. For example, the phenomenology of action, particularly Gallagher’s [11] work, provided the tools for unpacking intentional structure, bodily engagement, and the experiential dynamics of skilled activity, while the semiotics of performance [4, 10] helped articulate how actions are stylized, abstracted, or symbolically reframed when represented in games.

<!-- translation -->
这一转向打开了与多个理论传统对话的通道。例如，行动现象学，尤其是 Gallagher [11] 的研究，提供了分析意向结构、身体参与及熟练活动体验动态的工具；表演符号学 [4, 10] 则帮助说明，行动在游戏中得到表征时，怎样被风格化、抽象化，或以符号方式重新框定。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0045:paragraph-->
Adopting the terms’ action’ and ’activity’, rather than just refining our vocabulary, transformed the analysis itself. Instead of asking how a “game mechanic” works, we began asking how an activity is transmogrified into a game activity: What aspects of real lockpicking are preserved? Which are discarded? How do interface design, feedback systems, and thematic framing (cf. Järvinen’s [17] thematization) reshape the player’s sense of engagement? These questions cannot be meaningfully addressed within the conceptual scope of “mechanics,” which remains too coarse to capture distinctions between representation, embodiment, and systemic behaviour.

<!-- translation -->
采用“行动”与“活动”这两个术语，改变的不只是我们的用词，更是分析本身。我们不再询问某个“游戏机制”如何运作，而开始问，一种活动如何被变形为游戏活动：现实开锁的哪些方面得到保留？哪些被舍弃？界面设计、反馈系统与主题框架——参见 Järvinen [17] 的主题化概念——怎样重塑玩家的参与感？这些问题无法在“机制”的概念范围内得到有意义的处理，因为该概念仍过于粗糙，不能捕捉表征、具身性与系统行为之间的区别。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0046:paragraph-->
By the end of the project, the analytical value of abandoning “mechanics” was clear. The term’s supposed generality is a weakness: it collapses categories such as actions, procedures, representations, processes, that analytic work must keep distinct. Precision in terminology led to precision in analysis, enabling us to articulate how games construct activities rather than simply containing “mechanics.” In this context, holding onto game mechanics would have meant sacrificing explanatory clarity for a misleading sense of familiarity.

<!-- translation -->
项目结束时，放弃“机制”所具有的分析价值已很清楚。这个术语号称的一般性，恰好是其弱点：它把行动、程序、表征、过程等分析工作必须区分的范畴混在一起。术语的精确带来了分析的精确，使我们能够说明游戏如何建构活动，而非只是包含“机制”。在这个语境里，坚持使用游戏机制，将意味着为了某种误导性的熟悉感而牺牲解释的清晰性。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0047:paragraph-->
Thus, the analytical position mirrors the pedagogical one: game mechanics may serve as convenient shorthand in casual discourse, but it is too loose to support rigorous analysis. More fine-grained, contextually anchored concepts offer a clearer lens through which to understand how games structure action, experiences, and meaning.

<!-- translation -->
所以，分析层面的立场与教学层面的立场一致：游戏机制可以在日常话语中充当便利的简称，却过于松散，无法支撑严格分析。更细致、扎根具体语境的概念，能为理解游戏怎样组织行动、体验与意义提供更清晰的视角。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0048:paragraph-->
In sum, my position is that game mechanics remains too vague to serve as a reliable conceptual tool in either teaching or analysing games. Replacing this catch-all term with more precise, context-sensitive concepts (e.g. actions, activities, system behaviours, representational strategies) supports clearer thinking, more deliberate design practice, and more rigorous analytical insight.

<!-- translation -->
总而言之，我的立场是：游戏机制仍然过于含混，不能在游戏教学或分析中充当可靠的概念工具。以更准确、更依语境而定的概念——例如行动、活动、系统行为与表征策略——替换这个包罗一切的术语，将有助于更清楚的思考、更自觉的设计实践，以及更严格的分析洞见。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0049:heading-->
### 3.2 Espen Aarseth

<!-- translation -->
### 3.2 Espen Aarseth
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0050:paragraph-->
Game mechanics (hereafter simply mechanics) as a term became popularised in a series of workshops at the Game Developers Conference in 2001-2004, and then published in a paper by Hunicke et al. [14]. Their MDA framework is an ontological description of games as consisting of mechanics (the basic game elements), dynamics (the runtime consequences of the mechanics) and aesthetics (the players’ emotional responses). Prior to that, game ontologists and game designers had used various terms to describe the basic components, but mechanics stuck and overtook the popularity of previous terms, such as rules.

<!-- translation -->
“游戏机制”（下文简称“机制”）作为术语，通过 2001—2004 年游戏开发者大会的一系列工作坊流行起来，随后见于 Hunicke 等人发表的论文 [14]。他们的 MDA 框架从本体论层面描述游戏，认为游戏由机制（基本游戏元素）、动态（机制运行时的后果）和美感（玩家的情感反应）组成。在此之前，游戏本体论研究者和设计者曾用各种术语描述基本组成部分，但“机制”留了下来，其流行程度超过了“规则”等先前术语。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0051:paragraph-->
Given the overwhelming success of the term, one would expect it to have a clear and demonstrable analytic power, and not just fill a missing need for a catchy name. But what is a (game) mechanic? How do we know how to identify one? For instance, is shooting a virtual gun in a first person shooter one mechanic (firing the gun), two mechanics (aiming and clicking) or three mechanics (aiming, triggering, and a virtual bullet hitting something and causing an effect)?

<!-- translation -->
鉴于这一术语取得压倒性的成功，人们本应期待它具有明确且可展示的分析能力，而不只是满足对一个抓耳名称的需要。但什么是一个（游戏）机制？我们如何知道怎样辨认它？例如，在第一人称射击游戏中开一把虚拟枪，究竟是一个机制——开枪，两个机制——瞄准与点击，还是三个机制——瞄准、扣动扳机，以及虚拟子弹命中某物并产生效果？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0052:paragraph-->
The MDA paper gives two different definitions, “the particular components of the game, at the level of data representation and algorithms” (p. 2); “the various actions, behaviours and control mechanisms afforded to the player within a game context” (p. 3); and also a long list of examples: [fps]weapons, ammunition and spawn points, [golf]balls, clubs, sand traps and water hazards, [military strategy] tech and skill trees, a variety of enemy unit types, and levels or areas with variable ranges of mobility, visibility and field of view. At the same time, the MDA framework suggests another set of game components, in addition to the mechanics: “the game’s content (levels, assets and so on)”. It also mentions ‘rules’ in passing twice, but does not attempt to clarify the difference or overlap between the two terms.

<!-- translation -->
MDA 论文给出了两个不同定义：“游戏的具体组成部分，位于数据表征和算法的层次”（第 2 页）；“游戏情境中向玩家提供的各种行动、行为与控制机制”（第 3 页）。它还列出一长串例子：[第一人称射击]武器、弹药与出生点；[高尔夫]球、球杆、沙坑与水障碍；[军事策略]科技与技能树、多种敌方单位，以及移动范围、能见度和视野各不相同的关卡或区域。与此同时，MDA 框架又提出机制之外另一组游戏组成部分：“游戏的内容（关卡、素材等等）”。它还顺带两次提到“规则”，却没有尝试澄清这两个术语的差别或重叠。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0053:paragraph-->
After the spread of such a poorly defined term, researchers rushed in to define it properly, as they often do. But this hopeful ambition fails to consider the possibility that a good definition may not be possible, because the target phenomenon may not exist. When a term is introduced, it is not a given that there is a clear set of phenomena for it to describe. “Mechanics” is a metaphor unless used on clearly mechanical devices, and while some game components can be said to be mechanical, say, a paintball gun trigger; most are metaphorical, say a virtual fps gun trigger, and many are simply not well described by the term, say paintball balls, or “ammo” in a fps. Ammo is a resource, not a mechanic.

<!-- translation -->
这样一个定义不清的词流传开来后，研究者照例蜂拥而至，试图给它下一个恰当定义。然而，这种充满希望的雄心，没有考虑到一个好定义或许根本不可能成立，因为目标现象可能并不存在。引入一个术语，并不意味着必然存在一组清楚的现象供它描述。除非用于明确的机械装置，“机制”就是一种隐喻；某些游戏组成部分确实可以说是机械性的，例如彩弹枪的扳机，但大多数属于隐喻，例如第一人称射击中的虚拟扳机，还有很多根本不适合用这个词描述，例如彩弹球，或第一人称射击中的“弹药”。弹药是资源，不是机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0054:paragraph-->
If the goal is to target all “components of the game” (MDA), then ‘mechanics’ fail to offer an analytic label for most of them. Take a game space or a ‘level’, such as the 8x8 squares of a chess board, or a Counterstrike level like de_dust2. Are these mechanics, or not? According to the first MDA definition, they are, and according to the second, they are not. To call a chess board and the de_dust2 map ‘content,’ as the MDA paper implicitly does, is to ignore the very obvious structural affordances these spatial components have. And yet, they do not fit well under the ‘mechanics’ rubric. Nor is the attempt to ‘other’ mechanics vis-a-vis “the game’s content” helpful, as there are many different forms of structural aspects, and many forms of ‘content’, and many that overlap in fuzzy ways, such as visual puzzles. If we follow the ludo-ontological distinction between structure and representation, the levels fall under both, as a level has both representational and structural traits.

<!-- translation -->
如果目标是指称所有“游戏的组成部分”（MDA），那么，“机制”无法为其中大多数提供一个分析标签。以游戏空间或“关卡”为例，比如国际象棋棋盘的 8×8 个方格，或者《Counterstrike》的 de_dust2 地图。它们算不算机制？按照 MDA 的第一个定义，算；按照第二个定义，不算。像 MDA 论文隐含做的那样，把棋盘与 de_dust2 地图叫作“内容”，就忽略了这些空间组成部分显而易见的结构性可供性。然而，它们又不太适合被纳入“机制”名下。把机制与“游戏的内容”相对而加以“他者化”的尝试，同样没有帮助，因为结构方面有许多不同形式，“内容”也有许多形式，两者常以模糊方式重叠，例如视觉谜题。如果遵循游戏本体论中结构与表征的区别，关卡便同时属于两者，因为关卡兼有表征特征与结构特征。

To determine whether ‘mechanics’ can be salvaged as an analytical term, it is not enough to turn away from the MDA’s definitions and prop up a better one; one must first produce an overview or catalogue over the non-representational aspects of games, and only thereafter examine whether this catalogue contains a delimitable subset of operative components that would fit the term mechanics. Operative components come in many different sizes and shapes, from the simulation of physics to the configurations of the avatar to the decision-making algorithm of a non-playable character (NPC), and many, many more. Is the choice of NPC sidekick in a roleplaying game (RPG) a mechanic? Is using a cheat code a mechanic? Unless a definition can produce a clear answer to these questions, it is not a useful definition. The solution, instead, is to identify different structural elements of games, some operational and some temporal, transactional, spatial, social, physical, etc., and with this more concrete repertoire at hand, fuzzy notions like mechanics should not be needed.

<!-- translation -->
要判断“机制”能否作为分析术语挽救下来，不能只是转身放弃 MDA 的定义，再扶起一个更好的定义；必须先列出游戏非表征方面的概览或目录，然后才考察其中是否包含一组可界定的、适合称为机制的操作性组成部分。操作性组成部分形状大小各异，从物理模拟，到化身配置，再到非玩家角色（NPC）的决策算法，远不止这些。在角色扮演游戏（RPG）中选择 NPC 同伴，算不算机制？使用作弊码算不算机制？一个定义如果不能清楚回答这些问题，就不是有用的定义。因此，解决办法是辨认游戏中各种不同的结构元素：有些是操作性的，有些属于时间、交易、空间、社会、物理等方面。有了这套更具体的工具，“机制”这类模糊概念便应不再必要。

To explain this hypothesis, we need to invoke the notion of formal notions introduced by Edmund Husserl in his Logical Investigations. Husserl differentiates between material notions that contain empirical content (which makes them much easier to define) and formal notions that indicate the ontological category but contain no other content [15]. Examples of formal notions are words such as ”object”, ”feature”, ”thing”, ”action”, ”aspect”, ”function”, ”part”, ”process”, or ”something”. Categorial words can seem indispensable because they help us construct general sentences, disambiguate anaphors, or narrow the context, but at the same time, they can be perceived as ”empty”.

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0071:paragraph-->
Treating ”game mechanics” as a categorial term explains some of the abovementioned puzzles. Even though the term is ubiquitously used, it is notoriously hard to pinpoint. It is also sometimes possible to omit the word ”mechanics” – especially in cases where the context is obvious. In some cases, the term could just as well be replaced by one of the other formal concepts. This explains the surprising number of possible definitions of ”game mechanics” in the academic literature.

<!-- translation -->
把“游戏机制”视为范畴性用语，就能解释上面若干难题。这个词虽然无处不在，却出名地难以确定所指；这毫不奇怪，因为“方面”“特征”或“部分”等词也是如此。有时还可以省略“机制”一词，尤其是在语境显而易见时。某些情况下，这个术语同样可以用另一个形式概念替换。视作者认为哪些术语更基础而定，形式概念既可以被当作基本术语，也可以用其他基本术语来定义。这就解释了学术文献里“游戏机制”何以有数量惊人的各种可能定义。

We cannot possibly stop the trend of people using such terms casually and flexibly, because it’s part of our vocabulary. For academic research, game design practice, and regulatory purposes, what is important then is not to define what ‘game mechanics’ means, but to always define exactly what that ‘unit of interest’ one is discussing is. For example, what is a ‘loot box’ within the meaning of this specific research paper, design document, policy, etc. By calibrating our understanding at the beginning, we can ensure there is no misunderstanding.

<!-- translation -->
“游戏机制”的意思是：游戏内部一个受到关注的单元。它确切意味着什么，可能随语境改变。我们不可能阻止人们随意、灵活地使用这些词，因为它们是我们词汇的一部分。对于学术研究、游戏设计实践和监管目的而言，重要的并非界定“游戏机制”是什么意思，而是始终精确定义自己正在讨论的那个“受到关注的单元”。例如，在这篇特定研究论文、设计文档或政策中，“战利品箱”的含义是什么？在开头校准理解，就能确保没有误解。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0086:heading-->
### 3.5 Benjamin Horn

<!-- translation -->
### 3.5 Benjamin Horn
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0087:paragraph-->
My engagement with the term mechanics primarily manifested through the Beyond Game Mechanics Conference, at the beginning of which I contended that the term mechanics might still be profitably put to work in the study of games. By the end of the event, however, I had arrived at the same conclusion as the majority in attendance: that mechanics was a word that meant too many things to too many people, and that therefore it would be better to no longer use it for the study of games. The following statement will elaborate.

<!-- translation -->
我对机制一词的关注，主要体现在 Beyond Game Mechanics 会议上。会议开始时，我还认为，游戏研究或许仍能有效利用“机制”。然而，活动结束时，我已与多数与会者得出相同结论：机制一词对太多人意味着太多东西，因此，最好不再用它研究游戏。下面的陈述将作进一步说明。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0088:paragraph-->
It could be seen from the variety of topics at the conference just how much heavy lifting the term mechanics was being forced to do. For instance, Yahuai Lu discussed mechanics in regards to the historical gameplay of Pentiment (Obsidian Entertainment, 2022), Morgan Yu Hao talked about the mechanics of the combat in the Wuxia genre, Dora Cheng Zhang analysed the narrative mechanics of the Nier series, Payen Sylvain asked if freeze frames might be considered as mechanics, whereas Pan Xin thought that mechanics could be thought of as the knowledge a gamer might have. The diversity of conversations and interests indicates the remarkable, yet frustrating breadth of the term.

<!-- translation -->
从会议主题的多样性就能看出，机制一词被迫承担了多么沉重的工作。例如，Yahuai Lu 从《Pentiment》（Obsidian Entertainment，2022）的历史性玩法出发讨论机制；Morgan Yu Hao 谈论武侠类型的战斗机制；Dora Cheng Zhang 分析《Nier》系列的叙事机制；Payen Sylvain 询问定格画面能否算作机制；Pan Xin 则认为，机制可以被理解为玩家可能拥有的知识。这些对话和兴趣的多样性，显示这个术语范围之宽广，既令人惊异，又令人沮丧。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0089:paragraph-->
The topic of my talk was a close reading of the “mechanics” of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 [33]. This roleplaying game has a moderately unique combat system that contains both real-time and turn-based elements. Specifically, players control up to three characters in a battle, and each character acts on their individual turn. Players are able to select from a number of actions for their characters. Once they have selected an action, combat switches to real-time, and players must make a number of timing-based button inputs. On enemy turns, players also need to make timing-based button inputs in order to block or dodge powerful attacks.

<!-- translation -->
我的报告主题，是细读《Clair Obscur: Expedition 33》[33] 的“机制”。这款角色扮演游戏有一套颇为独特的战斗系统，同时包含即时与回合制元素。具体来说，玩家在一场战斗中控制最多三名角色，每个角色分别在自己的回合行动。玩家可以为角色从多种行动中选择；一旦选定，战斗便转为即时模式，玩家必须进行一连串有时机要求的按键输入。在敌人的回合，玩家同样需要把握时机按键，以格挡或闪避强力攻击。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0090:paragraph-->
While not entirely unique - there is precedent for such combat systems as may be seen in titles such as Paper Mario [16] or The Legend of Dragoon [36] - Expedition 33’s combat nevertheless distinguishes it from most games of its genre, which typically opt for either real-time or turn-based rather than the combination. I became intrigued with thoughts of how the phenomenological experience of the combat might impact the player’s interpretation or reading of the game. Similar to how a well-written sentence in a novel might contain techniques such as alliteration or metaphor; does the combination of real-time/turn-based combat change how a player might interpret Expedition 33?

<!-- translation -->
尽管并非完全独特——《Paper Mario》[16] 与《The Legend of Dragoon》[36] 等作品已有类似战斗系统的先例——《Expedition 33》的战斗仍使它区别于同类型的大多数游戏，后者通常在即时与回合制之间择一，而不将两者结合。我开始好奇：战斗的现象学体验，会怎样影响玩家对游戏的解释或解读？正如小说中一个写得很好的句子可能使用头韵、隐喻等技巧，即时与回合制战斗的结合，是否改变了玩家解释《Expedition 33》的方式？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0091:paragraph-->
Therefore, in an attempt to begin answering the above question, I resolved to propose a new definition of mechanics to describe my observations of Expedition 33’s combat. Somewhat glibly, I noted during my talk this would be at least the 50th known definition following the account of Lo et al (2021): a full half-century of different definitions.

<!-- translation -->
因此，为了开始回答上述问题，我决定提出一个新的机制定义，描述我对《Expedition 33》战斗的观察。我在报告中有些轻率地说，按照 Lo 等人（2021）的统计，这至少将是已知的第 50 个定义：整整半百种不同定义。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0092:paragraph-->
With reference to Aarseth and Grabarczyk [1] and Vargas-Iglesias and Navarrete-Cardero [38], I identified a distinction between “rules” and “mechanics”. Then, building from Hunicke et al [14], Järvinen [17], and Sicart [35], I circumscribed my understanding of mechanics to refer to the human players rather than including computers that might be playing the game as well. Inspired by my experiences playing Expedition 33 and by an observation from Sicart - “for game analysis, this suggests the possibility of closely studying the relationship between input device design, and player actions…” - I defined my mechanics as “the physical inputs from players designed for interaction with the game state” whereas rules would “include the methods by which computer agents interact with the game state.” Mechanics would be for players, rules for computer agents. Mechanics would specifically refer to player inputs - the clicking of buttons.

<!-- translation -->
参照 Aarseth 与 Grabarczyk [1]，以及 Vargas-Iglesias 与 Navarrete-Cardero [38]，我区分了“规则”和“机制”。随后，在 Hunicke 等人 [14]、Järvinen [17] 和 Sicart [35] 的基础上，我把自己对机制的理解限定于人类玩家，而不包括可能同样在参与游戏的计算机。受游玩《Expedition 33》的体验，以及 Sicart 某个观察的启发——“对于游戏分析，这意味着可以细致研究输入设备设计与玩家行动之间的关系……”——我把机制定义为“玩家为了与游戏状态互动而进行的物理输入”，规则则“包括计算机行动者与游戏状态互动的方法”。机制属于玩家，规则属于计算机行动者。机制具体指玩家的输入，也就是点击按钮。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0093:paragraph-->
Key to my definition of mechanics, and touched upon in my talk, was a distinction between trivial and non-trivial physical inputs. After all, players are always pressing buttons. Therefore, to be more specific, my definition of mechanics referred to the non-trivial pressing of buttons. For instance, the timing-based button presses of Expedition 33 would be non-trivial, whereas the clicking of a UI element in a more traditional turn-based combat system such as Final Fantasy X [37] would be trivially simple, and therefore would not be considered as a mechanic as such. This meant, and was quickly pointed out, that by my stricter definition turn-based games such as Final Fantasy X would have no mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
我的机制定义的关键，也是报告中谈到的一点，是区分平凡与非平凡的物理输入。毕竟，玩家一直在按按钮。因此，更具体地说，我的机制定义指的是非平凡的按键。例如，《Expedition 33》中需要把握时机的按键属于非平凡输入；而在《Final Fantasy X》[37] 这样的较传统回合制战斗系统中，点击一个用户界面元素则平凡而简单，因此不能算作机制。这就意味着——也很快被别人指出——按照我这个更严格的定义，《Final Fantasy X》之类的回合制游戏将没有机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0094:paragraph-->
Yet this would lead to a clear divergence between common understanding of the term mechanics and my own idiosyncratic definition and usage of it. I concurred that this was the case, admitting it could potentially cause further confusion. A participant then asked: why not simply come up with a new term to describe what I had observed in Expedition 33? It was agreed that what I was talking about could be an interesting topic for further investigation; the portmanteau “ludo-kinesthetics” was offered as a possible replacement. Why, after all, use mechanics, a word that comes laden with so much baggage?

<!-- translation -->
然而，这会使“机制”的通常理解与我个人特立独行的定义和用法明显分离。我同意这一点，也承认这可能进一步制造混淆。于是，一位参与者问：为什么不干脆创造一个新词，描述我在《Expedition 33》中观察到的东西？大家同意，我所讨论的现象可能是值得进一步研究的有趣主题；有人建议用合成词“ludo-kinesthetics”（游戏动觉）替代。毕竟，为什么非要使用“机制”这个背负如此多包袱的词？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0095:paragraph-->
Herein lays the crux of the matter. Attempting to use the term mechanics is a devilish choice. One can take the term mechanics as it is commonly understood, but then it is too vague and broad to be of much theoretical value; it can essentially mean whatever one wants it to mean in pointing to some component of the game under analysis. Alternatively, one can create one’s own more specific definition, but then why not just create or find another word for the specific thing one is trying to define? Thus I have arrived at the position that mechanics is a term not fit for the study of games, and I would urge other scholars and researchers to avoid use of it.

<!-- translation -->
症结就在这里。试图使用机制一词，是一种棘手的选择。我们可以照通常的理解使用它，但它又太含混、太宽泛，几乎没有理论价值；指向所分析游戏的某个组成部分时，它基本可以意味着使用者想让它意味着的任何东西。另一条路是，创造自己的、更具体的定义；可既然如此，为什么不干脆为那个想要定义的具体事物创造或寻找另一个词？所以，我得出的立场是，机制并不是适合游戏研究的术语，我也会敦促其他学者与研究者避免使用它。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0096:heading-->
## 4 Discussion

<!-- translation -->
## 4 讨论
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0097:paragraph-->
Though the methodological pathways followed by each author may be disparate, there is a clear and logical synthesis between the arguments. Aarseth lays the groundwork by establishing the problematic origins of the term. Grabarczyk and Xiao prove that philosophically and legalistically, “mechanics” is a word empty of meaning. Holopainen and Horn demonstrate the issues that arise from attempting to productively apply the term “mechanics” in both design and analytical contexts.

<!-- translation -->
虽然各位作者采取的方法路径不同，其论证却可以形成清楚而合乎逻辑的综合。Aarseth 通过确立这一术语起源中的问题，奠定了基础。Grabarczyk 与 Xiao 从哲学和法律角度证明，“机制”是一个意义空洞的词。Holopainen 与 Horn 则展示了，试图在设计和分析语境中有效运用“机制”时会出现哪些问题。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0098:paragraph-->
There are several important deductions that can be made from these arguments. First, the authors concur that “game mechanics” is a term that has resisted defining. As has been discussed at length, despite numerous attempts there is still no definition that the majority of the literature refers to; indeed, this was the sparking concern that inspired this paper. The lack of shared understanding has led to a profusion of preferred or idiosyncratic terms that cover a wide spectrum of meanings, resulting in analytical misunderstandings.

<!-- translation -->
可以从这些论证得出几项重要推论。第一，作者们一致认为，“游戏机制”一直是难以定义的术语。如前文详细讨论的，尽管尝试众多，仍没有任何定义被大多数文献共同采用；事实上，正是这一最初的关切触发了本文。共同理解的缺乏，导致各种偏好的或个人化的术语大量增生，覆盖广泛意义，并造成分析中的误解。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0099:paragraph-->
Second, the authors agree that part of the challenge of defining “mechanics” is as a consequence of its nature as a categorial term. As Grabarczyk points out, categorial terms are notoriously difficult to define (e.g. “part”), yet they can serve as useful shorthand when they are commonly understood. If “game mechanics” is a categorial term as the authors believe, then it might also explain why it has persistently been used across the last two decades even with a lack of clear definition. Furthermore, envisioning mechanics as a categorial term offers a pattern of usage for others to follow: instead of writing papers about mechanics as such, or creating new definitions for mechanics, the term acts instead as shorthand for a particular game component and nothing more.

<!-- translation -->
第二，作者们一致认为，定义“机制”的部分困难源于它作为范畴性用语的性质。正如 Grabarczyk 所指出的，范畴性用语出了名地难以定义，例如“部分”；不过，当人们对其有共同理解时，它们可以充当有用的简称。如果“游戏机制”确如作者们所认为的，是范畴性用语，那么，这也可能解释了为什么它在缺乏清晰定义的情况下，仍在过去二十年间持续使用。此外，把机制视为范畴性用语，也为其他人提供了一种可以遵循的使用模式：不再撰写讨论机制本身的论文，或为机制建立新定义，而只把这个词作为游戏某个特定组成部分的简称，仅此而已。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0100:paragraph-->
Where the authors diverge is on the question of whether or not the term should still see active use in the study of games. There are essentially two views on the matter. The first is that even though the term has caused debate and its meaning is somewhat nebulous, it is by and large harmless in its usage. It does not necessarily add much to the discussion of games, nor does it detract from it, and therefore whether or not it is used can largely be ignored. Therefore, mechanics should not necessarily be the focus of any analysis and the term should not be overused or laden with special meaning; it can and should only serve as a kind of shorthand for facilitating conversations. It should never be in the spotlight.

<!-- translation -->
作者们的分歧在于，游戏研究是否仍应主动使用这个术语。对此基本有两种看法。第一种认为，尽管它引发争论，意义也多少模糊，但总体而言，其使用无害。它不一定给游戏讨论增加多少东西，也没有使之损失什么，所以，是否使用它大体可以不予理会。因此，机制不应必然成为分析焦点，这个词也不应被滥用，或承载特殊意义；它只能、也应该只是促进交流的一种简称，永远不该站到聚光灯下。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0101:paragraph-->
The second perspective maintains that the term has caused too much confusion in its two decade tenure, and that it would be better to abstain from it altogether in favour of other, clearer terminology. The challenge, here, is determining suitable alternatives. If we are to eliminate “mechanics,” then we need to develop a more robust language of terminology for the entire complex of activities/actions/systems to which the current term refers. How many “game mechanics” actually exist when firing a gun, or picking a lock, or in how combat actions are taken? These would need to be broken down. Additionally, there is the risk that a replacement term for mechanics could suffer from the same pitfalls. For example: something like “game actions” could be vague enough that it ends up becoming, as “mechanics,” similarly categorial in nature.

<!-- translation -->
第二种观点坚持认为，这个词在二十年的使用期内已经造成太多混淆，最好干脆弃用，转而采用其他更清晰的术语。这里的挑战是确定合适的替代词。如果要消除“机制”，就需要为目前这个词所指的整套活动／行动／系统，建立更有力的术语语言。开枪、开锁，或采取战斗行动时，究竟存在多少个“游戏机制”？我们必须把它们拆解开来。此外，替代词也可能遭遇同样的陷阱。例如，“游戏行动”这样的表达可能足够模糊，最终像“机制”一样，变成具有类似范畴性质的词。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0102:paragraph-->
This paper, then, has identified the issues and challenges of game mechanics. Where can further research go from here? First, a systematic corpus analysis of the current usage of the term across not just academia [23] but also in the game industry and among player communities would help to confirm or disconfirm the hypothesis that mechanics is categorial by nature, perhaps supported by a linguistic dissection of the word, or potentially perspectives from the philosophy of language. An empirical experiment could be productive in this regard: if the use of the term “game mechanics” was expressly forbidden, what sort of alternatives would naturally arise to fill the categorial niche?

<!-- translation -->
因此，本文辨明了游戏机制的问题与挑战。后续研究可以往哪里去？首先，对这个词当前的用法开展系统语料分析，不仅涵盖学术界 [23]，还涵盖游戏产业与玩家社群，将有助于确认或否定“机制在性质上属于范畴性用语”的假说；这或许还可以得到词语本身的语言学剖析，或语言哲学视角的支持。在这一点上，实证实验可能颇有成效：如果明令禁止使用“游戏机制”，会自然产生哪些替代表达来填补这个范畴位置？
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0103:paragraph-->
Then, there is the question of possible replacements for “game mechanics,” should it be decided to have done with the term entirely. Holopainen mentions such terms as “representational strategies” and “systematic behaviours” as possibly being able to offer more precise language for analysis and design. Perhaps with the structural analysis suggested by Aarseth to offer more theoretical scaffolding and precise delimitation of meaning, one or more of these terms could offer starting points for moving definitively beyond game mechanics.

<!-- translation -->
然后，假如决定彻底弃用这个术语，还存在“游戏机制”的可能替代词问题。Holopainen 提到“表征策略”和“系统性行为”等术语，可能为分析与设计提供更精确的语言。如果再结合 Aarseth 建议的结构分析，提供更多理论支撑并精确划定意义，其中一个或多个术语或许能成为出发点，帮助我们明确地超越游戏机制。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0104:heading-->
## 5 Concluding Remarks

<!-- translation -->
## 5 结语
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0105:paragraph-->
This paper presents an argument for the reconceptualization of game mechanics. The authors unanimously agree that the current definitions of the term are too vague and broad to act as the foundations for game analysis and criticism. Conversely, by establishing game mechanics as a categorial term, this paper lays the groundwork for a clear pattern of usage – or indeed, for it to not be used at all.

<!-- translation -->
本文提出了重新构想游戏机制的论证。作者们一致同意，这个术语现有的定义过于含混和宽泛，不能充当游戏分析与批评的基础。反过来，通过确立游戏机制作为范畴性用语的性质，本文为一种清晰的使用模式——乃至彻底不再使用它——奠定了基础。
<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:VGM0106:paragraph-->
Since its inception, game mechanics has existed in a grey area that has consequently resulted in its use being irregular and disjointed. This paper calls for moving beyond this murky conceptual paradigm and towards a more systematic understanding of the complexities of its usage.

<!-- translation -->
游戏机制自诞生起就处于灰色地带，因而导致其用法不规则、彼此割裂。本文呼吁超越这种晦暗的概念范式，对其使用的复杂性取得更系统的理解。
<!-- endpair -->
