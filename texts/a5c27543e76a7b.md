# 简介

论文介绍一款女巫召唤游戏的原型：系统先给出答案关键词，再由本地大语言模型反向生成村民的不同问题；玩家用一次文字输入提出同时解决所有问题的物品。原型结合语言模型、相似度评估模型、扩散图像生成与Unity，以此探索人工智能原生游戏的机制设计。作者报告游戏创作马拉松中的初步反馈，并提出创造力、用户体验和本地／云端能耗比较的研究方法；系统性用户研究与能耗比较仍属于后续工作。

## Abstract

This paper presents "One Spell Fits All", an AI-native game prototype where the player, playing as a witch, solves villagers’ problems using magical conjurations. We show how, beyond being a standalone game, "One Spell Fits All" could serve as a research platform to explore several key areas in AI-driven and AI-native game design. These areas include AI creativity, user experience in predominantly AI-generated content, and the energy efficiency of locally running versus cloud-based AI models. By leveraging smaller, locally running generative AI models, including LLMs and diffusion models for image generation, the game dynamically generates and evaluates content without the need for external APIs or internet access, offering a sustainable and responsive gameplay experience. This paper explores the application of LLMs in narrative video games, outlines a game prototype’s design and mechanics, and proposes future research opportunities that can be explored using the game as a platform.

## 1. Introduction

<!-- translation -->
## 1. 引言

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00015:paragraph -->
In "One Spell Fits All" (OSFA), players take on the role of a witch, capable of conjuring an infinite variety of items using text-based input (see Figures 1 and 2). NPC-Villagers visit the witch with a wide range of problems, and the player is tasked with finding exactly one solution that solves all of their problems at once – using a single text input. The game uses locally running AI models to generate and evaluate game content, providing a sustainable alternative with lower latency compared to cloud-based AI services. Furthermore, we believe that this video game can serve as a research platform, as the game can provide an environment to investigate various aspects of generative AI, such as AI creativity, user experience for AI-generated content, and energy efficiency – all of which can be compared across different models or implementations. This paper discusses the game’s integration of generative AI tools and outlines the potential research directions that can be pursued using this platform.

<!-- translation -->
在《一咒皆解》（OSFA）中，玩家扮演一位女巫，能够通过文字输入召唤出种类无穷的物品（见图1和图2）。NPC村民带着各式各样的问题拜访女巫，玩家的任务是找到恰好一个方案，用一次文字输入同时解决他们的所有问题。游戏采用在本地运行的人工智能模型来生成和评估游戏内容，相较于云端人工智能服务，提供了一种延迟更低的可持续替代方案。此外，我们认为这款电子游戏可以充当研究平台，因为它能够提供一种环境，用以考察生成式人工智能的各个方面，例如人工智能创造力、人工智能生成内容的用户体验，以及能源效率；这些方面均可在不同模型或实现方式之间进行比较。本文讨论游戏如何整合生成式人工智能工具，并概述可以利用这一平台开展的潜在研究方向。

<!-- pair:en:OSF00016:heading -->
## 2. Related Work

<!-- translation -->
## 2. 相关工作

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00017:paragraph -->
Incorporating AI into interactive narratives has been a long-term objective in video game research [1]. This goal has seen significant advancements with the recent surge in generative AI tools, particularly large language models (LLMs). Furthermore, the use of LLMs in video games has been shown to increase the amount and the quality of possible player interactions [2, 3, 4, 5].

<!-- translation -->
将人工智能纳入交互叙事一直是电子游戏研究的长期目标[1]。随着近年来生成式人工智能工具，尤其是大语言模型（LLM）的兴起，这一目标的实现取得了显著进展。此外，已有研究表明，在电子游戏中使用大语言模型，能够增加玩家可能进行的交互数量，并提高交互质量[2, 3, 4, 5]。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00018:paragraph -->
Significant advances for generative AI integrated into games have been shown with examples like "1001 Nights" [6, 7], a game which explores dynamic content generation by creating images of weapons based on the stories told by the player. The authors of the game also argue for the use of the term ”AI-native” games, as opposed to AI-powered games, for video games that use generative models as an integral core feature. We adopt this term to describe OSFA.

<!-- translation -->
将生成式人工智能整合进游戏的显著进展，已经由《1001 Nights》等实例展现出来[6, 7]。这款游戏根据玩家讲述的故事创建武器图像，以此探索动态内容生成。该游戏的作者还主张：对于把生成模型作为不可或缺的核心特征的电子游戏，应使用“人工智能原生”游戏这一术语，而非“人工智能赋能”游戏。我们采用这个术语来描述OSFA。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00019:paragraph -->
The integration of AI in video games has been explored in literature, with foundational texts such as those by Yannakakis and Togelius [8] providing a comprehensive overview of how artificial intelligence can enhance game design through techniques like procedural content generation (PCG), player modelling, and dynamic difficulty adjustment. OSFA builds upon these concepts by applying local AI models to create a responsive and sustainable game environment. Elements of PCG [9] have formed the base of OSFA, to showcase how AI can autonomously create complex and engaging game content.

<!-- translation -->
人工智能在电子游戏中的整合已经受到文献讨论。例如，Yannakakis与Togelius的基础性著作[8]全面概述了人工智能如何通过程序化内容生成（PCG）、玩家建模和动态难度调整等技术来改进游戏设计。OSFA以这些概念为基础，应用本地人工智能模型，创建响应及时且可持续的游戏环境。PCG的若干要素[9]构成了OSFA的基础，用来展示人工智能如何自主创建复杂而引人投入的游戏内容。

## 3. Game Design and Mechanics

<!-- translation -->
## 3. 游戏设计与机制

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00021:paragraph -->
The core gameplay loop of OSFA revolves around the player’s text input. Taking on the role of a witch, players have to address the various needs of NPC-villagers by using magical conjurations. In each round, the system feeds a solution item from a list of predefined solutions to the LLM, which then reverse-engineers problems. These problems are then posed as requests from the villagers. Players have complete creative freedom regarding their input, which is funnelled into various models.

<!-- translation -->
OSFA的核心玩法循环围绕玩家的文字输入展开。玩家扮演女巫，必须运用魔法召唤来回应NPC村民的各种需求。每轮中，系统从预先定义的解决方案列表中取出一个物品作为答案，交给大语言模型，再由模型反向构造问题。这些问题随后以村民请求的形式呈现出来。玩家在输入方面拥有完全的创作自由，这些输入会被送入各种模型。

<!-- pair:en:OSF00026:heading -->
### 3.2. Problem and Solution Generation

<!-- translation -->
### 3.2. 问题与解决方案的生成

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00027:paragraph -->
One of the innovative aspects of OSFA is the use of AI to dynamically generate problems and evaluate solutions at run-time. The game currently employs the Mistral-7B-Instruct model, via the LLM for Unity library 1 to generate unique problems equal to the number of present villagers, based on a provided keyword. This ensures that the challenges presented to the player are varied, contextually relevant, and have at least one correct solution for all problems. Once the player provides a solution, the game tests for validity and then uses the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 2 transformer model to evaluate the solution based on a similarity score to the original keyword. Future iterations of the game will try the use of LLMs for evaluation of the solution as well. The game then generates a visual representation of the solution, using a custom diffusion model 3 and ComfyUI 4, which is then presented to the player and the villagers.

<!-- translation -->
OSFA的一个创新之处，是利用人工智能在运行时动态生成问题并评估解决方案。游戏目前通过LLM for Unity库[1]使用Mistral-7B-Instruct模型，依据给定的关键词，生成与在场村民数量相同、各不相同的问题。这样可以确保呈现给玩家的挑战多样、符合语境，而且至少存在一个能够解决全部问题的正确方案。玩家给出方案后，游戏先检验其有效性，再使用all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Transformer模型[2]，依据该方案与原始关键词的相似度分数来评估它。游戏后续版本还会尝试使用大语言模型来评估解决方案。随后，游戏借助定制扩散模型[3]和ComfyUI[4]，生成解决方案的视觉表现，并呈现给玩家和村民。

### 3.3. Villager Interactions

<!-- translation -->
### 3.3. 村民交互

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00033:paragraph -->
The interactions between the player and the NPC-villagers are central to the game’s narrative and mechanics. Villagers respond to the player’s solutions based on the AI’s evaluation, and their satisfaction is reflected in the game’s scoring system. The scoring system, in turn, affects how many villagers come back to the player. These interactions are designed to create a dynamic and responsive gameplay loop, in which the player must think creatively and strategically to meet all of the villagers’ needs.

<!-- translation -->
玩家与NPC村民之间的交互，是游戏叙事与机制的核心。村民根据人工智能的评估对玩家的解决方案作出回应，他们的满意程度会体现在游戏的计分系统中。计分系统反过来影响有多少村民再次来找玩家。这些交互旨在构成一个动态且响应及时的玩法循环；玩家必须创造性地思考，并作出策略安排，才能满足所有村民的需求。

<!-- endpair -->

### 3.4. Artistic Elements

<!-- translation -->
### 3.4. 美术元素

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00035:paragraph -->
OSFA features a distinct pixel art style that complements its AI-driven gameplay. The game’s art is a mix of free assets and AI-generated art, such as the sprite of the witch. The locally running model creates pixel art assets in real-time based on player input, which matches stylistically with the rest of the game. Furthermore, the background music and the theme song for the main menu have also been created using generative AI tools.

<!-- translation -->
OSFA具有鲜明的像素美术风格，与其人工智能驱动的玩法相呼应。游戏美术混合使用免费素材和人工智能生成的美术，例如女巫的精灵图。运行于本地的模型依据玩家输入实时创建像素美术素材，其风格与游戏的其他部分相匹配。此外，背景音乐和主菜单的主题曲也由生成式人工智能工具创作。

## 4. Technical Workflow

<!-- translation -->
## 4. 技术工作流

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00037:paragraph -->
Figure 3 illustrates the workflow of OSFA, where various AI components generate and evaluate game content dynamically in the game’s core loop.

<!-- translation -->
图3展示了OSFA的工作流：在游戏的核心循环中，各个人工智能组件动态生成和评估游戏内容。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00038:paragraph -->
The workflow is detailed as follows:

<!-- translation -->
工作流详述如下：

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00039:list -->
1. Villagers enter the witch’s store, bringing their unique problems for the witch (the player) to solve.

<!-- translation -->
1. 村民进入女巫的商店，带来各自独特的问题，等待女巫（玩家）解决。

## 5. AI Integration

<!-- translation -->
## 5. 人工智能整合

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00046:paragraph -->
OSFA uses multiple AI models running locally and at the same time to create a dynamic gameplay experience. The integration of these models is central to the game’s ability to generate problems, evaluate solutions, and provide visual feedback. This section gives more details on how various AI components are implemented and interact within the game. Notably, the game’s architecture was designed with modularity in mind – which means that each model can be swapped out separately.

<!-- translation -->
OSFA采用多个在本地同时运行的人工智能模型，创造动态的游玩体验。这些模型的整合，是游戏能够生成问题、评估解决方案和提供视觉反馈的关键。本节进一步说明各个人工智能组件如何在游戏中实现及相互作用。需要特别指出，游戏架构在设计时考虑了模块化，这意味着各个模型均可单独替换。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00047:paragraph -->
The Mistral-7B-Instruct model, used as the LLM within the game, is responsible for generating the unique problems that each villager presents to the player. The model operates locally on the player’s computer, limiting energy consumption and eliminating the need for external API calls. The use of such a local implementation can also reduce the latency between player input and feedback, as well as improve data privacy concerns, as no data is sent to third parties.

<!-- translation -->
游戏中的大语言模型Mistral-7B-Instruct，负责生成每位村民向玩家提出的独特问题。该模型在玩家电脑上本地运行，从而限制能源消耗，并免去外部API调用。由于不向第三方发送数据，这种本地实现还能够缩短玩家输入与反馈之间的延迟，并改善数据隐私方面的问题。

To evaluate the player’s solutions, OSFA integrates the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 transformer model. Currently, all villagers’ problems are evaluated at once, but future implementations will aim to evaluate them on a case-by-case basis (e.g., by using an LLM instead of a transformer), thus allowing for partially correct answers as well.

<!-- endpair -->

依据玩家输入，召唤出的物品由一个像素美术扩散模型和定制的ComfyUI工作流生成。该工具被整合进游戏的人工智能流水线，使游戏能够依据输入实时、动态地创建像素美术素材。

By running the AI components locally, the game minimizes latency and increases security through the lack of network communication, although the player’s hardware specification can still cause delays.

<!-- translation -->
OSFA的人工智能模型实现在一个支持实时交互与处理的框架中。模型在玩家机器上本地运行；游戏使用Unity游戏引擎制作，通过脚本、插件和库来控制人工智能模型。在本地运行人工智能组件，可以最大限度降低延迟，并因不进行网络通信而提升安全性，不过玩家的硬件配置仍然可能造成延迟。

### 7.1. AI Creativity

<!-- translation -->
### 7.1. 人工智能创造力

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00057:paragraph -->
Creativity, especially in the context of generative AI, is a rich and interdisciplinary research field [10, 11, 12]. As OSFA uses an LLM to generate villagers’ wishes and requests based on specific keywords, we believe that it opens up new possibilities for exploring AI creativity. The ability of AI to generate novel and varied problems from similar inputs is understood to be a form of creativity, presenting several interesting research questions. The first step could be defining and quantifying AI creativity in the context of the game prototype. One approach could be to develop a creativity score to evaluate the LLM’s performance in generating unique and contextually appropriate problems. This score would be based on the similarity of problems generated by the AI across various playthroughs and different versions of the game.

<!-- translation -->
创造力，尤其是生成式人工智能语境中的创造力，是一个丰富的跨学科研究领域[10, 11, 12]。由于OSFA使用大语言模型，依据特定关键词生成村民的愿望和请求，我们认为，它为探索人工智能创造力开辟了新的可能。人工智能能够从相似输入中生成新颖而多样的问题，这种能力被理解为创造力的一种形式，提出了几个有趣的研究问题。第一步可以是在这一游戏原型的语境中定义并量化人工智能创造力。一种做法是建立创造力分数，用于评估大语言模型生成独特且符合语境的问题的表现。这个分数将以人工智能在多次游玩和游戏不同版本中生成的问题之间的相似度为基础。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00058:paragraph -->
To explore this, data on AI-generated problems needs to be collected across multiple sessions and then compared against several types of solutions: those manually crafted by human designers, those generated solely by the LLM, and a mix of human and AI-generated content. This comparison could reveal how closely AI outputs align with expected creative standards, as well as how AI can complement human creativity.

<!-- translation -->
为探索这一点，需要收集多次游玩中人工智能生成的问题数据，再将其与几类方案比较：由人类设计者手工创作的方案、完全由大语言模型生成的方案，以及人类与人工智能共同生成的内容。这样的比较可以揭示，人工智能输出在多大程度上符合预期的创造力标准，以及人工智能如何补充人类创造力。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00059:paragraph -->
In addition to a creativity score, other metrics can help to better understand and measure AI creativity. Novelty can assess how often the AI produces entirely new problems not seen in previous playthroughs. Diversity measures the range of different problem types generated in response to similar keywords within a single playthrough. Consistency can evaluate the AI’s ability to maintain high-quality, contextually relevant problems across different scenarios. Adaptability assesses how well the AI adjusts its problem generation to varying game contexts or player actions, ensuring that the problems remain appropriate and challenging.

<!-- translation -->
除了创造力分数，其他指标也有助于更好地理解和衡量人工智能创造力。新颖性可以衡量人工智能生成此前游玩中从未出现的全新问题的频率。多样性衡量同一次游玩中，针对相似关键词所生成的不同问题类型的范围。一致性可以评估人工智能在不同情境下维持高质量、符合语境的问题的能力。适应性评估人工智能能否根据不同游戏情境或玩家行动，适当地调整问题生成，确保问题仍然合适且富有挑战。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00060:paragraph -->
This leads to another question – how do different LLM models compare in terms of their creative outputs? By systematically comparing these metrics across various LLMs, we could gain insights into which models are better suited for generating creative content in games. This could also inform the future development of AI systems designed specifically for creative tasks. Developing these metrics and comparing different models should deepen our understanding of how AI can be used to generate novel and engaging content. This research could have broader implications for AI applications in creative industries, such as game design, storytelling, and digital art.

<!-- translation -->
这又引出另一个问题：不同的大语言模型在创造性输出方面表现如何？通过系统比较不同大语言模型在这些指标上的表现，我们可以了解哪些模型更适合为游戏生成创造性内容。这也可以为专门面向创造性任务的未来人工智能系统开发提供信息。开发这些指标、比较不同模型，应能加深我们对于如何使用人工智能生成新颖且引人投入的内容的理解。这项研究可能对游戏设计、故事讲述、数字艺术等创意产业中的人工智能应用产生更广泛的影响。

### 7.3. Energy Efficiency of Local AI Models vs. Cloud-Based Solutions

<!-- translation -->
### 7.3. 本地人工智能模型与云端方案的能源效率

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00065:paragraph -->
As AI-native games like OSFA continue to develop, an important question arises: How does the utilization of local AI models compare to cloud-based solutions in terms of energy efficiency? This question is particularly relevant for our game, which operates AI models locally. While it is generally accepted that running smaller LLMs locally would be faster and more power efficient than remote calls to larger cloud-based models (such as GPT-5), this claim still warrants further investigation, especially in terms of broader scalability and environmental impact. A more detailed comparative analysis could explore the trade-offs between local and cloud-based solutions, considering the quality of the generated content, latency, and also the total energy consumption involved. This includes power requirements for both local machines and cloud-based servers, as well as the energy costs of data transmission between clients and remote servers. The goal would be to determine which approach offers greater energy efficiency, considering factors such as scalability, environmental impact, and the trade-offs between performance, quality, latency, and energy use.

<!-- translation -->
随着OSFA这样的人工智能原生游戏继续发展，一个重要问题随之出现：在能源效率方面，使用本地人工智能模型与采用云端方案相比如何？这个问题与我们的游戏尤其相关，因为游戏在本地运行人工智能模型。虽然人们普遍认为，在本地运行较小的大语言模型，会比远程调用较大的云端模型（例如GPT-5）速度更快、耗电更少，但这一主张仍需进一步研究，尤其要考察更广泛的可扩展性与环境影响。更详细的比较分析可以探索本地与云端方案之间的取舍，将生成内容的质量、延迟及涉及的总能源消耗纳入考虑。这包括本地机器和云端服务器的电力需求，以及客户端与远程服务器之间数据传输的能源成本。目标是判断哪一种方式具有更高的能源效率，同时考虑可扩展性、环境影响，以及性能、质量、延迟与能源使用之间的取舍等因素。

<!-- endpair -->

<!-- pair:en:OSF00066:paragraph -->
Understanding these differences can have significant implications for game development. If local AI models prove to be more energy-efficient while guaranteeing sufficient quality of experience, they could become the preferred choice for sustainable game design. However, if their quality remains lacking, and cloud-based solutions offer better efficiency, this will influence how AI resources are deployed in future projects as well. Exploring the energy efficiency of AI models is important for many reasons – not only for improving game performance but also for advancing sustainability in the AI and video game industry. By comparing the energy use of local AI models with server-based and cloud-based solutions, this research could guide future developments in AI-driven game design, promoting more environmentally conscious practices.

<!-- translation -->
理解这些差异，可能对游戏开发产生重大影响。如果事实证明，本地人工智能模型在保证足够体验质量的同时更节能，它们就可能成为可持续游戏设计的首选。但如果它们的质量仍然不足，而云端方案的效率更高，这同样会影响未来项目部署人工智能资源的方式。探索人工智能模型的能源效率，有诸多重要原因：既是为了改善游戏性能，也是为了推进人工智能与电子游戏产业的可持续性。通过比较本地人工智能模型与基于服务器、基于云端的方案的能源使用，这项研究可以为未来人工智能驱动的游戏设计提供指引，推动更具环境意识的实践。
